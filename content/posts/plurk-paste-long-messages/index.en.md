<a href="http://www.plurk.com"><strong>Plurk</strong></a> is a well-known microblogging system in Taiwan. Each message is limited to 140 characters. That limit makes things awkward for people who want to ask the community a question or share something good. For example, say you see a really funny story, but it's 300 characters long, so you can't post it on Plurk. Some people solve this by splitting the 300 characters into three parts and replying to their own post with each part, but that's a bit inconvenient. Here I want to introduce a new site called <strong><a href="http://paste.plurk.com/">Plurk Paste</a></strong>


<!--more-->
Plurk Paste lets you paste articles, stories, code, and other content onto its site. It then gives you a URL, and all you need to do is paste that URL into Plurk. I'm not sure whether this site is run by Plurk itself. The interface is clean and there are no ads at all. And if you post code, it automatically processes the code, which makes it easier for readers.

<strong>URL: <a href="http://paste.plurk.com/">http://paste.plurk.com/</a></strong>
<h2><strong>Plurk Paste Tutorial</strong></h2>
1.Go to the Plurk Paste site and paste the message you want to share into the big box. If you're pasting code, choose the code's language from the drop-down menu. If your language isn't in the drop-down, choose Text(preformatted), not Text, or your code formatting will get messed up.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/2011-01-07_151415.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-4378" title="2011-01-07_151415" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/2011-01-07_151415-486x500.jpg" alt="" width="486" height="500" /></a>

2.Once you've set things up and pasted your content, click the Paste button.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/2011-01-07_151510.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-4379" title="2011-01-07_151510" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/2011-01-07_151510-485x500.jpg" alt="" width="485" height="500" /></a>

3.It will take you to another page. Copy that page's URL and paste it into Plurk, and you're done.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/2011-01-07_151640.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-4380" title="2011-01-07_151640" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/2011-01-07_151640-520x416.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="416" /></a>

4.Here's what Plurk Paste looks like in action. When you click a Plurk Paste link, it doesn't open a new web page. Instead, the content is displayed right on Plurk.
