It feels like it was the day before yesterday.

Songshan Vocational School new student training!!

Then the class lists came out too. I'm in the Data Processing Department, Class 15, Number 4~

In short, the new student training was a blast.

Except for the part at the very beginning where the principal rambled on, which made me regret going to the training a little.

But after I got to know my classmates, it was never boring.

We chatted and had a great time, and the teachers didn't even stop us.


<!--more-->


My classmates all seem pretty friendly.

Oh, except for one girl whose name I can't remember. She sent me an IM saying "we're not close, don't add me," so I deleted her right away.

Everyone else seems very friendly and approachable~ (Especially the girls, so many pretty ones~~)

Our class's guys even put together a team of pretty girls as class officers. People say France has a cabinet of beautiful women, and now Songshan Vocational has a class of pretty officers.....

As a result, a lot of the girls seem a bit unhappy? Because they don't want to be officers 0.0

And then, there really are so many pretty girls~!!

But the school year starts a little too early.

It starts a week early, which means school starts next Tuesday. I'm so excited!

Next Monday, I have to go to school to register and pick up my books.

I'd really like to ask whether some of the books can be skipped, since I already know some of the material. For example, I already know VB, so there's no need to buy that textbook.

Also, there's the thing......

My summer homework isn't done yet, is it?

The book reports, ugh~

Songshan Vocational doesn't give much homework, but I still don't feel like doing it= =

I also didn't expect Songshan's college entrance rate to be so high.

The principal said it's 30%. I believe him, since they've posted the data. Unlike Ren'ai Junior High, which keeps saying it's 5th in all of Taipei= =

But when I looked it up online, it's not even in the top 10?

Last time my younger brother went to Ren Junior High for new student training, he heard the principal talking nonsense there.

Our school really has 30%~~

That's much better than Yucheng High School, the one at No. 30.

Yucheng High School doesn't even reach 20%....... I only found out today, when I went out to play with classmates, that a 30% (almost 40%) college entrance rate is actually high.

All in all, Songshan Vocational is awesome.

It's convenient to get to, the teachers are good, and the facilities aren't old.

Tuition is cheap too.

A friend told me private schools charge NT$30,000 in tuition, and uniforms cost NT$8,000= =

Songshan Vocational's tuition is NT$10,000 (tuition plus miscellaneous fees), and the uniforms are only NT$4,000......

And that classmate of mine has two kids who both go to private schools. That's really not something an ordinary family can afford!
