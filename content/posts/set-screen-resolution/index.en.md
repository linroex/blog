Today I'll teach you how to change the screen resolution.

It can also be described as the screen size.

I'm not very good at explaining, so...

Anyway, changing this changes what's shown on your screen <span style="color: #0000ff;">(bigger or smaller)</span>

It's useful for people who find the screen hard to read.


<!--more-->


step1: Go to the desktop, right-click, and choose <span style="color: #ff0000;">"Properties"</span>

<img src="http://nwufiq.bay.livefilestore.com/y1p3B5wiZJRQfejFImjEQ1lGHocWQADX5iVkICK8tJdTXf_FLchIaAfNqu8PvFcBZwS_69PQOTx_x6osN9E7FcLP0V-d5RXnQs1/2009-05-08_190750.jpg" alt="" />

step2: After you click Properties, a new window opens. Select the <span style="color: #ff0000;">"Settings"</span> tab in that window.

<img src="http://nwufiq.bay.livefilestore.com/y1p3B5wiZJRQfcmZK1R3-6Pb5aqjdx1TmmMXCsF4iau_w_vSRPIdCy62eYauGEALuQDOxON4Ka7asZxojClcpTOSX6JEBXNvAJU/2009-05-08_190920.jpg" alt="" />

step3: On the Settings tab, you'll see a <span style="color: #ff0000;">"Screen resolution"</span> box <span style="color: #0000ff;">(see the red box in the image below)</span>.

Drag the resolution slider with your mouse, and the screen resolution will appear below it <span style="color: #0000ff;">(see the blue box in the image below)</span>.

<img src="http://nwufiq.bay.livefilestore.com/y1pbndSI7PaxrR_aWIa23zLV79Rt8rAaKJYMoVj7ZFJRBX1VOCLImayjD084kYUeTNpWTRCl71Yu6u_nGHqKLf9WcBmuN6fmdC7/2009-05-08_191125.jpg" alt="" />

step4: After you've selected the resolution you want, you can click <span style="color: #ff0000;">"Apply"</span> to try it out <span style="color: #ff6600;">(it's normal for the screen to go black for a moment)</span>
<img src="http://nwufiq.bay.livefilestore.com/y1p4mmvMqIeDzPxIJ641toOW_76c2q0Irh-Fs2W7Ma_d4knu7p9qGEpQP6gVlpQchAfo6ylH1dXelPYPrgJYPDb0JAT_U8uTB01/2009-05-08_191530.jpg" alt="" />

step5: After applying the new resolution, a window appears asking whether to keep the settings. If you like the new resolution, click <span style="color: #ff0000;">"Yes"</span>.

<img src="http://nwufiq.bay.livefilestore.com/y1p4mmvMqIeDzM6KVJTRnhEpB0Rvd-YLs62uW65ceCmo-ohw9azTmVWKKnJbmRoQub_5EVPq8SMHA68ygWJUmsp0yqGm51qWyP9/2009-05-08_191623.jpg" alt="" />

And that's it!
