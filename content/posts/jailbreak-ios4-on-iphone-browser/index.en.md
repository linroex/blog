No joke, this time it's a real one-click jailbreak. From start to finish, you only need to press one button to complete the jailbreak. You don't even need to download any jailbreak program. All you have to do is open a web page on your iPhone or iPod Touch. It's really amazing. When I saw the news this morning about this magical jailbreak method, I didn't believe it. But just now I tested it on my own iPod Touch (sweating nervously the whole time...), and the result is real!! However, this web-based jailbreak method <strong>currently only works on iOS 4</strong>, and it may stop working very soon. Also, don't worry that this web jailbreak program will sneak a Trojan horse onto your iPhone. I had that concern too at first, but then I saw that it's a product of the famous iPhone Dev Team, so I felt better~


<!--more-->
The method is very simple. Take out your iPhone or iPod Touch and connect to <a href="http://www.jailbreakme.com/">http://www.jailbreakme.com/</a>, then slide the slider to the right, and it will start jailbreaking automatically. Don't touch the device during this process, or I can't say what will happen.... Also, a US court recently ruled that jailbreaking is legal. The only catch is that you lose warranty eligibility after jailbreaking (screw it xD), so everyone doesn't need to worry about being sued for jailbreaking. But it seems nobody has actually worried about it...

After the jailbreak finishes, the system will automatically install Cydia for you, and you can download the software you want from there. Also, it seems iOS 4 can't run the Backgrounder multitasking tool yet, because I couldn't download it when I tried. But themes (WinterBoard) work, and OpenSSH is definitely a must-install~

Must-install apps after the jailbreak:
<ul>
	<li>OpenSSH (lets you access the system folders of your iPhone or iPod Touch)</li>
	<li>WinterBoard (theme software)</li>
	<li>SBsetting</li>
	<li>iAccess (a better input method app)</li>
	<li>Usb Drive (turns your iPhone or iPod Touch into a flash drive)</li>
</ul>
