<strong>Gmail</strong> is my favorite email service, and there are plenty of reasons why. The main one is simple: Gmail's spam filtering is very accurate. I've never seen Gmail mark a real email as spam. I've been using it for more than two years, and spam has never worried me. Gmail's storage isn't unlimited like Yahoo's, but it keeps growing. In fact, the current capacity is so large that you won't use it up in a lifetime, so there's no need to worry about a full inbox.
Login page: <a href="http://mail.google.com/mail"> http://mail.google.com/mail</a>


<!--more-->

<h3>Top 10 advantages of Gmail:</h3>
<ol>
	<li>Supports POP3 mail retrieval</li>
	<li>Filters</li>
	<li>Can be extended with other features (for example, as online storage)</li>
	<li>Very accurate spam detection</li>
	<li>Automatic email forwarding</li>
	<li>Changeable themes</li>
	<li>Mobile access</li>
	<li>Convenient email sorting</li>
	<li>Conversation stacking (emails from the same sender are grouped together)</li>
	<li>Nearly 8G of huge storage</li>
</ol>
In this post, I'll show you how to:
<ol>
	<li>Create a Gmail account</li>
	<li>Send email and attach files</li>
	<li>Use Gmail labels (for organizing)</li>
	<li>Set up an auto-reply</li>
	<li>Add a signature</li>
	<li>Change the theme</li>
	<li>Change your display name</li>
</ol>
<h2>1. How to Create a Gmail Account</h2>
1. Go to the Gmail <a href="http://mail.google.com/mail" target="_blank">sign-in page</a> and click the big Create account button.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_110327.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-2192" title="2009-11-08_110327" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_110327.jpg" alt="2009-11-08_110327" width="491" height="354" /></a>

2. Fill in your details. The username you want becomes your account name. After you enter your information, don't forget the verification code. Then click [I accept. Create my account].

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_111244.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-2194" title="2009-11-08_111244" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_111244.jpg" alt="2009-11-08_111244" width="491" height="354" /></a>

3. If the screen below appears, your account has been created. Click [Get started] to enter Gmail.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_111200.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-2193" title="2009-11-08_111200" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_111200.jpg" alt="2009-11-08_111200" width="491" height="354" /></a>

<h2>2. Sending Email with Gmail</h2>
The first time you use Gmail, you might have trouble finding where to compose an email, because the option is text rather than a button, so it isn't very obvious. Here's how to send an email with Gmail!

1. Click [Compose] in the upper left.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_111742.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2195" title="2009-11-08_111742" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_111742-500x360.jpg" alt="2009-11-08_111742" width="500" height="360" /></a>

2. Next you'll see the compose window you're probably familiar with. Type the recipient's address in the [To] field. To send to several people at once, separate the addresses with commas (,). Then type a subject in the [Subject] field.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_112103.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2196" title="2009-11-08_112103" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_112103-500x360.jpg" alt="2009-11-08_112103" width="500" height="360" /></a>

3. Now write your message. Gmail has a very handy editor, so you don't have to deal with the HTML yourself. You can create all kinds of effects right in Gmail's editor (bold, italic, text color, and so on).

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08-11-42-21.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2197" title="2009-11-08 11 42 21" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08-11-42-21-500x360.jpg" alt="2009-11-08 11 42 21" width="500" height="360" /></a>

4. You can also click [Attach files] below the subject and select the files you want to include. I believe the limit is 5M per file.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08-11-45-00.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2198" title="2009-11-08 11 45 00" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08-11-45-00-500x360.jpg" alt="2009-11-08 11 45 00" width="500" height="360" /></a>

5. When you've finished writing, click [Send], and it's on its way.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08-11-46-09.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2199" title="2009-11-08 11 46 09" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08-11-46-09-500x360.jpg" alt="2009-11-08 11 46 09" width="500" height="360" /></a>

6. Gmail itself doesn't offer a way to insert images, but you can check Gmail Labs. I remember there's a feature for it. The detailed steps are:

Click [Settings] → [Labs], then use search (Ctrl+F) to find the [Insert image] feature. Change its setting from Disable to Enable, and finally click [Save Changes].

Now go back to composing an email, and you'll see a new Insert image button in the editor.

<h2>3. Gmail Labels</h2>
Gmail's built-in labels, combined with filters, make it easy to organize your mail. I sorted thousands of emails in only 10 minutes. Let me show you how to use labels.
<h4>I. Creating a label:</h4>
Of course, the first step is to create a label to use!

1. Click [Settings] → [Labels], then scroll down to the [Labels] section (not the system labels).

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08-12-00-23.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2203" title="2009-11-08 12 00 23" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08-12-00-23-500x360.jpg" alt="2009-11-08 12 00 23" width="500" height="360" /></a>

2. Next to [Labels] you'll see a white box for creating a new label. Type a label name there and click [Create] to add it.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08-12-02-28.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2204" title="2009-11-08 12 02 28" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08-12-02-28-500x360.jpg" alt="2009-11-08 12 02 28" width="500" height="360" /></a>

3. Then you can choose whether this label appears in the sidebar of your inbox (area A in the screenshot). If you want it there, click [Show] next to the label.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08-12-04-32.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2205" title="2009-11-08 12 04 32" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08-12-04-32-500x360.jpg" alt="2009-11-08 12 04 32" width="500" height="360" /></a>

II. Adding labels to emails

Adding labels to one email or many isn't hard. It became even easier after Gmail's big label overhaul. You can drag and drop, or use the traditional method.

1. Select the emails you want to label. You can select many at once.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_130222.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2206" title="2009-11-08_130222" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_130222-500x360.jpg" alt="2009-11-08_130222" width="500" height="360" /></a>

2. Click [Labels], then choose the label you want to apply.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_130731.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2207" title="2009-11-08_130731" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_130731-500x360.jpg" alt="2009-11-08_130731" width="500" height="360" /></a>

3. If it worked, the text shown in the box will appear.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_130754.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2208" title="2009-11-08_130754" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_130754-500x360.jpg" alt="2009-11-08_130754" width="500" height="360" /></a>

You can also add a label by dragging. As before, first select the emails. Then drag the label from the sidebar onto one of the selected emails.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_131241.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2209" title="2009-11-08_131241" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_131241-500x360.jpg" alt="2009-11-08_131241" width="500" height="360" /></a>

<h2>4. Turning On the Vacation Responder</h2>
The vacation responder is for when you're too busy to check your inbox often. When someone sends you an email, it automatically replies for you. Handy, right?

1. Click <span style="background-color: #ffffff;">[Settings] → [General]</span> at the top of Gmail.

<span style="background-color: #ffffff;"><a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_131739.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2210" title="2009-11-08_131739" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_131739-500x360.jpg" alt="2009-11-08_131739" width="500" height="360" /></a></span>

<span style="background-color: #ffffff;">2. Scroll down to find [Vacation responder]. Select [Vacation responder on] next to it, then enter a subject and message for your reply. Finally, click [Save Changes].</span>

<span style="background-color: #ffffff;"><a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_132154.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2211" title="2009-11-08_132154" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_132154-500x360.jpg" alt="2009-11-08_132154" width="500" height="360" /></a></span>

<span style="background-color: #ffffff;">3. While the vacation responder is on, a banner at the top of your inbox reminds you that it's active.</span>

<span style="background-color: #ffffff;"><a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_132328.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2212" title="2009-11-08_132328" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_132328-500x360.jpg" alt="2009-11-08_132328" width="500" height="360" /></a></span>

<h2><span style="background-color: #ffffff;">5. Setting Up a Signature</span></h2>
<span style="background-color: #ffffff;">Almost every email provider has a signature feature. Does Gmail? That might sound like a silly question, since Gmail has everything the others do! Here's how to set up a signature.</span>

1. Click <span style="background-color: #ffffff;">[Settings] → [General]</span> at the top of Gmail.

<span style="background-color: #ffffff;"><a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_131739.jpg"><img style="border: 0px initial initial;" title="2009-11-08_131739" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_131739-500x360.jpg" alt="2009-11-08_131739" width="500" height="360" /></a></span>

<span style="background-color: #ffffff;">2. Scroll down to the signature section, select the option next to "No signature," then type your signature. Finally, click [Save Changes] as before.</span>

<span style="background-color: #ffffff;"><a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_134445.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2213" title="2009-11-08_134445" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_134445-500x375.jpg" alt="2009-11-08_134445" width="500" height="375" /></a></span>
<h2><span style="background-color: #ffffff;">6. Changing the Gmail Theme</span></h2>
<span style="background-color: #ffffff;">Another feature that sets Gmail apart is the ability to change themes. Everybody gets tired of things eventually. You get bored with toys after playing with them too long, and even the same girlfriend gets old after a while XD (they say there's a scientific basis for wanting something new!). Anyway, you may have noticed that some of the screenshots in this tutorial have a stone background. That's the theme I switched to~</span>

<span style="background-color: #ffffff;"><span style="background-color: #ffffff;">1. Click [Settings] → [Themes] at the top of Gmail, then choose a theme you like.</span></span>

<span style="background-color: #ffffff;"><span style="background-color: #ffffff;"><a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_135149.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2214" title="2009-11-08_135149" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_135149-500x360.jpg" alt="2009-11-08_135149" width="500" height="360" /></a></span></span>

<span style="background-color: #ffffff;"><span style="background-color: #ffffff;">2. If you choose a scenery theme, Gmail will ask for your location. Just fill it in as requested.</span></span>

<span style="background-color: #ffffff;"><span style="background-color: #ffffff;"><a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_135615.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2215" title="2009-11-08_135615" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_135615-500x360.jpg" alt="2009-11-08_135615" width="500" height="360" /></a></span></span>

<span style="background-color: #ffffff;"><span style="background-color: #ffffff;">3. If you want to make your own theme, that's easy too. Gmail offers a very simple design interface. Just scroll down and click [<span style="text-decoration: underline;"><span style="color: #ff0000;"><strong>Choose your favorite color</strong></span></span>].</span></span>

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_135711.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2216" title="2009-11-08_135711" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_135711-500x361.jpg" alt="2009-11-08_135711" width="500" height="361" /></a>
<blockquote>Note: Gmail's Themes feature doesn't work with every browser that Gmail supports. It only works with Internet Explorer 7.0+, Firefox 2.0+, Safari 3.0, and Google Chrome. If you're not using one of these browsers, the Settings page won't show the [Themes] tab. Please note that Themes don't work with Internet Explorer 6.0.

<h2><span style="background-color: #ffffff;"><span style="background-color: #ffffff;">7. Changing Your Display Name</span></span></h2>
<span style="background-color: #ffffff;"><span style="background-color: #ffffff;">When you email friends, your message shows the name you entered when you signed up. What if you don't want them to know your real name? Here's how to change it.</span></span>

<span style="background-color: #ffffff;"><span style="background-color: #ffffff;">1. Click [Settings] → [Accounts and Import], then click [Edit info] next to [Send mail as].</span></span>

<span style="background-color: #ffffff;"><span style="background-color: #ffffff;"><a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_140249.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2217" title="2009-11-08_140249" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/2009-11-08_140249-500x360.jpg" alt="2009-11-08_140249" width="500" height="360" /></a></span></span>

<span style="background-color: #ffffff;"><span style="background-color: #ffffff;">2. A new window will pop up. In the [Name] field there are two options. Choose the second one, type the nickname you want to use, and click [Save Changes].</span></span>
