我想很多人都有玩過遊戲吧,也知道很多單機遊戲啟動時往往需要安碟片才能,如果光碟損毀就不能玩遊戲,這時候大家應該都會想要備份一片吧!但是遊戲公司因為自身利益著想都會加上防拷措施,用普通方法是不能燒錄的,那............ 要怎麼辦? 今天這篇文章聽。風會交各位讀者如何利用酒精120來破解防拷設計
<h3>必備軟體:</h3>
Alcohol120 (30天試用版) <a href="http://mirror.free-downloads.net/3/Alcohol120_trial_2.0.0.1331.exe">官方載點</a> | <a href="http://www.virustotal.com/analisis/bdd052ba22e8354d6f1e549cbac52dd112a21b7b7ac78061b22c50f61d3486fc-1266279213">掃毒報告</a>

A-Ray Scanner (判斷防拷設計用) <a href="http://www.box.net/shared/cec6lrf77z">Box.net</a>| Xun6 | <a href="http://www.virustotal.com/analisis/c80bbb88678e4175fb147143cc6afc2b133ea420a659e80182850c8af790e955-1264509219">掃毒報告</a>

註:酒精這套程式是試用版的,只能使用30天,所以建議各位在安裝完30內完成破解的工作

準備好上面2套程式了嗎? 現在我們就一步步帶各位來學習吧


<!--more-->


要破解他的防拷設計第一步當然是要知道他用啥方式囉~在這裡我們推薦使用ArayScanner這套程式來判斷,因為他不但是免費程式,而且判斷很準確幾乎沒有誤判,還有它會連線到他們的資料庫來判斷,所以連最新出現的防拷設計都能判斷出來: )

<h3>三.酒精(Alcohol120)安裝教學</h3>
1.開啟酒精安裝檔然按一下NEXT

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_120041.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border-width: 0px;" title="2009-07-03_120041" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_120041_thumb.jpg" alt="2009-07-03_120041" width="507" height="397" border="0" /></a>

2.這裡是他程式的授權條款,你如果接受就按一下I Agree

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_120156.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border-width: 0px;" title="2009-07-03_120156" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_120156_thumb.jpg" alt="2009-07-03_120156" width="507" height="397" border="0" /></a>

3.他會問你是否要重開電腦,選擇"確定"就是要(沒重開不能安裝)

4.重開電腦以後他會自動跳出安裝程式(如果沒有跳出來就手動開啟)

一開始的步驟和前面一樣就是按NEXT然後按I Agree

接下來他會問你要不要安裝它的工具列,不想安裝的人就把它取消掉然後按NEXT

5.這裡是要你選擇需要安裝的元件,基本上維持預設然後按NEXT

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_122206.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border-width: 0px;" title="2009-07-03_122206" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_122206_thumb.jpg" alt="2009-07-03_122206" width="507" height="397" border="0" /></a>

6.如果你的C槽空間不夠的話,這裡可以讓你設定是否要把安裝位置改到其他地方,設定完以後按NEXT

<h3>四.酒精燒錄映像檔教學</h3>
1.開啟酒精主程式

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_122918.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border-width: 0px;" title="2009-07-03_122918" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_122918_thumb.jpg" alt="2009-07-03_122918" width="84" height="66" border="0" /></a>

2.因為是試用版的程式,所以打開以後要等10秒才能按下確定鍵

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_123008.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border: 0px;" title="2009-07-03_123008" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_123008_thumb.jpg" alt="2009-07-03_123008" width="566" height="417" border="0" /></a>

3.開啟以後要等他跑一下東西

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_123137.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border: 0px;" title="2009-07-03_123137" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_123137_thumb.jpg" alt="2009-07-03_123137" width="515" height="358" border="0" /></a>

4.選擇"映像檔製作精靈"

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_123220.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border: 0px;" title="2009-07-03_123220" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_123220_thumb.jpg" alt="2009-07-03_123220" width="515" height="357" border="0" /></a>

5.先設定你遊戲光碟所在的光碟槽(1)

然後在資料類型那裡選擇你剛剛用程式判斷出來的防拷方式

設定好以後按"下一步"

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_123426.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border: 0px;" title="2009-07-03_123426" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_123426_thumb.jpg" alt="2009-07-03_123426" width="475" height="387" border="0" /></a>

6..設定好映像檔要存放的位置,其他設定都不要亂動,然後按"開始"他就會開始燒錄了

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_123553.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border: 0px;" title="2009-07-03_123553" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_123553_thumb.jpg" alt="2009-07-03_123553" width="475" height="387" border="0" /></a>

7.製作好硬像檔以後你可以選擇把它掛載起來或燒到光碟裡,如果要燒錄到光碟裡的話按一下"映像檔燒錄精靈"就可以了

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_124123.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border: 0px;" title="2009-07-03_124123" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_124123_thumb.jpg" alt="2009-07-03_124123" width="515" height="357" border="0" /></a>

如果家裡沒有燒錄機也別擔心,酒精這套程式內建有虛擬光碟機的功能,只要再虛擬光碟機上面按一下右鍵選擇掛載映像檔即可

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_124257.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border: 0px;" title="2009-07-03_124257" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/32773f1bb0c6_8FFE/20090703_124257_thumb.jpg" alt="2009-07-03_124257" width="509" height="682" border="0" /></a>
