As the title says, this unboxing post is two days late. I bought this iPad on Thursday night (3/10), but then I got so busy that I didn't have time to write the unboxing post... Actually, I've wanted one since the iPad first came out, and I kept saving money until now, when there was a special offer~ So I went for it. Some people asked why I didn't buy the iPad 2, since it's cheaper and faster, right? I think the news media may have spread the wrong idea. As soon as the iPad 2 was announced, Apple cut the price of the iPad to a minimum of NT$12,900, while the iPad 2 was expected to cost around NT$16,000. Yes, the iPad 2 does perform better, but my own experience with the iPad 1 is that its performance is already good, and you rarely run into lag or crashes. If only you could turn off iOS multitasking...


<!--more-->
Apple products are already expensive. The iPad is one of those products where the main unit is relatively cheap, but the accessories are relatively expensive. For example, the most basic accessories, a screen protector and a case, can cost around NT$1,000 for both. If you buy higher-end stuff, it can reach NT$2,000. There's also the Bluetooth keyboard, which costs over NT$2,000. So I had no choice but to buy the iPad 1 and spend the leftover money on accessories~

Also, if you're planning to buy an iPad and you usually buy books at Eslite, get an Eslite accumulation card. The card tracks your spending, and once you reach NT$10,000, it upgrades to a membership card. After you get the accumulation card, buy the iPad at the Apple booth inside Eslite, and you'll become a member right away xD
