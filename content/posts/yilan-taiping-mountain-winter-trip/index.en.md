Early this morning my mom woke me up and said we were going to play at <a href="http://tps.forest.gov.tw/">Taipingshan</a> in Yilan. Originally we'd agreed to go on Saturday so we could stay overnight, but Saturday's weather still wasn't good enough = = it was drizzling a bit and the sun never came out, so we had no choice but to go for just one day on Sunday. Otherwise, besides staying a night, we could have seen the sea of clouds. My mom also said that if we were lucky, it would snow on <a href="http://tps.forest.gov.tw/">Taipingshan</a>. As it turned out, Taipingshan didn't get any snow this year — it was <a href="http://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%8D%97%E6%B9%96%E5%A4%A7%E5%B1%B1">Nanhu Mountain</a> next door that had snow, but it's too far away with mountains in between, so we couldn't go @@ and I heard the traffic jam over there was really bad. But today's weather was really nice too — the weather bureau finally got the forecast right XD. First time I've seen the sun since Chinese New Year.


<!--more-->

<a href="http://tps.forest.gov.tw/"><img src="http://lh4.ggpht.com/_Oa6EYoORRA4/S4ErAem5IsI/AAAAAAAAAf4/T7Tx2L7GAew/s400/DSCN0011.JPG" alt="" /></a>
I'd thought getting to <a href="http://tps.forest.gov.tw/">Taipingshan</a> would be quick — after all, the Hsuehshan Tunnel is open and there weren't many people heading to Yilan today, so there were no traffic jams along the way. But once we got to Yilan I realized it actually takes a long time to get there, because Taipingshan is really high..... The car drove for 2 hours just to get from Sanjiao to our destination — the entrance to the Taipingshan National Forest Recreation Area. For a full three hours my brother and I were basically in a coma, because the mountain road up <a href="http://tps.forest.gov.tw/">Taipingshan</a> has so many turns, winding and winding and winding..... By the end, even someone like me who's never gotten carsick (only seasick) couldn't take it anymore. The photos from Taipingshan helped me update my blog's layout, but I didn't capture any of the scenery along the road — in the end I only took pictures once I was on the Bong Bong Train.

While we were on <a href="http://tps.forest.gov.tw/">Taipingshan</a> we also went to ride some little train called the Bong Bong Train. At first I thought it was the bumper cars at an amusement park, but later I found out it was a little train. I still don't know why it's called the Bong Bong Train — all I know is that there was a black dog next to it that kept running after the train XD. You'll see in the photos below. The staff there said that black dog runs after the train almost every day, on every trip. My mom even fed it a zongzi. After the train reached the station we went walking on the forest trail, and we could see snow on Nanhu Mountain next door.

The drive back was probably the most intense part of the whole day, because the fog going down the mountain was incredibly thick. At first the fog wasn't too bad, so we went to <a href="http://www.goilan.com.tw/elake/">Cuifeng Lake</a>. But by the time we got there, the whole of Taipingshan had taken on that hazy, Shangri-la feel, and the fog was really heavy, so we had to hurry and drive back down — otherwise if we'd kept playing around we might not have been able to get down at all = = Even though we left early it was still scary, because the fog was so thick you couldn't even see the road clearly. What surprised me was that at a time like that there were still people wanting to drive up....... I don't know whether to call that reckless or what. Maybe they were going to stay at Taipingshan Villa! That's where my memory ends ~~~~ because I was so dizzy I couldn't stand it and automatically went into sleep mode. I didn't come back to normal until we reached flat ground.
