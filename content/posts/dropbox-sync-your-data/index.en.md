Calling Dropbox an online hard drive actually sells it a bit short, but it's the only name I can think of right now. If anyone has a better name, please let me know! Dropbox's main function is that you install a client program on your computer. When you have a file to upload, you just drop it into the Dropbox client and it's uploaded. Dropbox also gives free users 2GB of space, and if you recommend Dropbox to someone else, your space can grow too (up to 8GB). Personally, I feel 2GB is already enough.


<!--more-->


I first got into Dropbox because of my mom. She kept saying she wanted to find a place to back up her documents, but with an ordinary online hard drive there were too many steps, and she always forgot the upload part. Later, an internet friend (I don't even know who it was = =) sent me a Dropbox invitation, so I recommended it to my mom. After she tried it for a while, she also said it was very convenient XD. Dropbox has versions for Windows, Mac, Linux, and iPhone, so I can sync my data easily. But one word of warning: although Dropbox lets you share your files, don't expect to upload files of several hundred MB, because Dropbox is better suited to syncing documents and images. Uploading games would take a long time, and you might get disconnected halfway through.

<h2>Sending Dropbox Invitations</h2>
1.Go to the Dropbox website, enter your username and password in the upper right corner, and click "Log in."

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_000807.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3391" title="2010-06-26_000807" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_000807-550x334.jpg" alt="" width="550" height="334" /></a>

2.Click "Get Extra Space Free."

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_001129.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3392" title="2010-06-26_001129" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_001129-550x334.jpg" alt="" width="550" height="334" /></a>

3.There are three ways to invite people to join Dropbox. Each time someone you invite joins, you get 250MB of extra space.

Method 1 (A): Give others a direct link to invite them (this method is for instant messaging, forums, and blogs).

Method 2 (B): Let Dropbox search your contacts and send invitations to the people in them.

Method 3 (C): Manually enter the email addresses of the people you want to invite and send the invitations.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_001441.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3393" title="2010-06-26_001441" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_001441-550x334.jpg" alt="" width="550" height="334" /></a>

The invitation process is simple. I'll only cover sending invitations through your contacts, since I think method A needs no explanation = =

1.Enter your email account and password. Note: you must enter the full email address. For example, my Gmail account is linroex, so I need to enter linroex@gmail.com. Then click "Retrieve your contacts."

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_001758.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3394" title="2010-06-26_001758" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_001758-550x334.jpg" alt="" width="550" height="334" /></a>

2.Opening your contacts

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_001806.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3395" title="2010-06-26_001806" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_001806-550x334.jpg" alt="" width="550" height="334" /></a>

3.Finally, click "Invite XX (number) friends" to send the invitations.
