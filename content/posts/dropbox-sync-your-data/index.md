其實稱DropBox為網路硬碟有點太小看它了,可是目前我也只想的到這個稱呼,有人有更好的叫法的話再告訴我吧~ Dropbox這個程式主要功能就是在你的電腦中裝一支客戶端程式,當你有檔案需要上傳時只要把檔案丟給Dropbox的客戶端程式就可以上傳了,另外Dropbox提供免費用戶2GB的空間,如果推薦別人加入Dropbox的話還可以讓你的空間變大(最多到8G),我自己是覺的2G已經夠用了


<!--more-->


當初會接觸到Dropbox是因為我媽,她一直說什麼要找個地方把他的文件備份起來,可是給他用一般的網路硬碟它又會嫌使用步驟太多,就是上傳那些的動作她會忘記,後來有個網友(我也不知道是誰= =)寄了一封Dropbox的邀請函給我,我就推薦給我媽用,後來看他用一用自己也覺得很方便的說XD,因為Dropbox除了有Windows版以外還有Mac.Linux跟iPhone版,可以讓我很方便的同步資料,不過在這裡要做一下提醒,雖然Dropbox可以讓你分享你的檔案,但是別妄想把幾百MB的檔案上傳上去,因為Dropbox比較適合用來做文件與圖片的同步,上傳遊戲的話應該要很久,而且可能傳到一半你就斷線了

<h2>安裝Dropbox客戶端程式</h2>
1.先到Dropbox官網下載主程式,點選【Download Dropbox】即可

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-25_231018.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3380" title="2010-06-25_231018" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-25_231018-550x334.jpg" alt="" width="550" height="334" /></a>

2.下載好Dropbox之後打開他的安裝檔,然後按【執行】

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-25_232248.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3381" title="2010-06-25_232248" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-25_232248.jpg" alt="" width="404" height="283" /></a>

3.按一下【Install】

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-25_233328.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3382" title="2010-06-25_233328" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-25_233328.jpg" alt="" width="503" height="387" /></a>

4.安裝中

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-25_233421.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3383" title="2010-06-25_233421" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-25_233421.jpg" alt="" width="503" height="387" /></a>

5.如果你已經有Dropbox帳號的話就按【I alreadly have a Dropbox account】,如果沒有帳號的話就按【I don"t have a Dropbox account】然後按【Next】

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-25_234419.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3384" title="2010-06-25_234419" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-25_234419-459x400.jpg" alt="" width="459" height="400" /></a>

如果你剛剛選擇的是【I alreadly have a Dropbox account】,那你現在就要輸入你的帳號密碼然後按Next然後就結束囉~

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-25_234315.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3385" title="2010-06-25_234315" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-25_234315-459x400.jpg" alt="" width="459" height="400" /></a>

如果你剛選擇的是【I don"t have a Dropbox account】,那你就輸入你要的帳號密碼還有你的相關資訊,然後按Next再看下一步教學
<blockquote>以下是中翻英:

First name   姓氏

Last name    名字

E-mail            信箱(帳號)

password      密碼

verify password      確認密碼(再輸入一次密碼)</blockquote>
<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-25_235500.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3386" title="2010-06-25_235500" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-25_235500-459x400.jpg" alt="" width="459" height="400" /></a>

6.選擇2GB方案然後按Next

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-25_235618.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3387" title="2010-06-25_235618" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-25_235618-459x400.jpg" alt="" width="459" height="400" /></a>

7.這部分是教學,直接按Skip tour and finish

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-25_235815.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3388" title="2010-06-25_235815" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-25_235815-459x400.jpg" alt="" width="459" height="400" /></a>

8.維持預設值然後按Finish

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-25_235918.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3389" title="2010-06-25_235918" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-25_235918-459x400.jpg" alt="" width="459" height="400" /></a>

到這裡就完成安裝了,使用方法很簡單,先到桌面開啟Dropbox主程式,然後就可以開始用了,上傳檔案就直家把檔案丟到Dropbox的主程式(資料夾),使用方法跟Windows的檔案總管一樣

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_000427.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3390" title="2010-06-26_000427" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_000427-503x400.jpg" alt="" width="503" height="400" /></a>

P.S.檔案總管:你每天在那裏複製.移動和刪除檔案就是在使用檔案總管
<h2>寄送Dropbox邀請函</h2>
1.先進入到Dropbox官網,然後在右上角輸入帳密在按Log in

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_000807.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3391" title="2010-06-26_000807" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_000807-550x334.jpg" alt="" width="550" height="334" /></a>

2.按一下Get Extra Space Free選項

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_001129.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3392" title="2010-06-26_001129" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_001129-550x334.jpg" alt="" width="550" height="334" /></a>

3.你有3種方式邀請別人加入Dropbox,每邀請成功一個人你就可以獲得250MB的空間

第一種(A):直接給別人連結來做邀請(此方法是用於即時通.論壇和部落格)

第二種(B):讓Dropbox搜尋你的通訊錄,並且對通訊錄成員發出邀請函

第三種(C):由你手動填入要收邀請函的人的信箱再寄邀請函

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_001441.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3393" title="2010-06-26_001441" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_001441-550x334.jpg" alt="" width="550" height="334" /></a>

邀請方式很簡單,這裡只介紹用通訊錄寄邀請函,至於A方法我想不需要多做解釋吧= =

1.輸入你的信箱帳號跟密碼,請注意:一定要輸入完整的帳號,例如我的gmail帳號是linroex,就必須輸入linroex@gmail.com,然後按Retrieve your contacts

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_001758.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3394" title="2010-06-26_001758" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_001758-550x334.jpg" alt="" width="550" height="334" /></a>

2.正在開啟你的通訊錄

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_001806.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3395" title="2010-06-26_001806" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-26_001806-550x334.jpg" alt="" width="550" height="334" /></a>

3.最後,按一下Invite XX(數字) friends即可送出邀請函
