Send to Kindle is a Chrome extension that every Kindle user should install!! You often come across interesting articles or tutorials online. Some people bookmark them, others export them to PDF. If you have a Kindle, you have one more option: send the page to your Kindle. That's exactly what the Send to Kindle extension does.


<!--more-->


Send to Kindle delivers content to your Kindle by email, so you don't need to connect the Kindle to your computer with a USB cable. You only need to turn on the Kindle's internet connection. The extension also tidies up the page for you first, removing ads and keeping the important parts. You can also make one more round of edits yourself before sending.

<h2>Setting Up and Using Send to Kindle</h2>
Installing Send to Kindle doesn't mean you can use it right away. You need to make some settings first.

1. Open the Amazon website. At the very bottom of the page you'll find "Manage Your Kindle."

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_124513.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-5964" title="2012-01-24_124513" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_124513-520x481.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="481" /></a>

Log in to your Amazon account.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_124621.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-5965" title="2012-01-24_124621" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_124621-520x448.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="448" /></a>

2. On the left side of the page, find "Personal Document Settings" and click it. Then, under "Approved Personal Document E-mail List," add a new address: kindle@klip.me

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_124856.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-5966" title="2012-01-24_124856" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_124856-520x481.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="481" /></a>

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_124916.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-5967" title="2012-01-24_124916" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_124916-520x289.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="289" /></a>

3. Next, on the same page, look at the top for "Send-to-Kindle E-Mail Settings." Find the email address your Kindle uses to receive files, and copy it.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_125149.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-5968" title="2012-01-24_125149" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_125149-520x488.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="488" /></a>

4. Right-click Send to Kindle and choose "Options." In the field "My Kindle's email address:," enter the address you copied earlier. Enter only the part before the @.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_125405.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-5969" title="2012-01-24_125405" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_125405-520x488.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="488" /></a>

5. After making the settings, click "Save" to save them (you may need to scroll down the page a little).

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_125609.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-5970" title="2012-01-24_125609" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_125609-520x488.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="488" /></a>

6. Next, open the web page you want to send to your Kindle and click the Send to Kindle button. It automatically removes the unnecessary elements, and what you see on screen is what will be sent to your Kindle.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_125914.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-5971" title="2012-01-24_125914" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_125914-520x416.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="416" /></a>

On this screen you can also make edits, such as adding some text. Just click the part you want to change. Think of it as a simple version of Word rather than a web page.

7. Click Send, and it will be on its way. In my experience, it takes about 10 minutes to appear on your Kindle.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_130145.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-5972" title="2012-01-24_130145" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_130145-520x416.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="416" /></a>

Also note that in some cases, sending files to a Kindle may cost money. For details, see: <a href="http://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=200505520&amp;#fees">http://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=200505520&amp;#fees</a>
