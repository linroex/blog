Send to Kindle是一個Chrome外掛，而且他是一個Kindle用戶必裝的外掛！！ 很多時候你會在網路上看到一些有趣的文章、教學，這時候有的人會存成書籤，有的人會匯出成PDF檔，如果你有一台Kindle，那你就多了一個新的選擇，把它傳送到Kindle裡面，而Send to Kindle這個外掛所做的就是這麼一件事情


<!--more-->


Send to Kindle它是透過Email的方式把資料傳送到你的Kindle裡面，所以你不需要用USB把Kindle連接到電腦，只需要打開Kindle的網路就可以了，另外這個外掛還會先幫你編修網頁，把廣告去除掉，留下重要的地方，你也可以自己在編修一次，然後才會傳送出去
<h2>Send to Kindle資訊</h2>
<ul>
	<li>官方網站：<a href="http://www.klip.me/">http://www.klip.me/</a></li>
	<li>下載頁面：<a href="https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/ipkfnchcgalnafehpglfbommidgmalan/details" target="_blank">Chrome WebStore</a></li>
</ul>
<h2>Send to Kindle設定、使用</h2>
安裝好Send to Kindle以後並不代表你可以馬上使用它，你必須要先做好一些設定

1.開啟Amazon網站，在網頁最下方你可以找到「Manage Your Kindle」

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_124513.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-5964" title="2012-01-24_124513" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_124513-520x481.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="481" /></a>

登入你的Amazon帳戶

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_124621.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-5965" title="2012-01-24_124621" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_124621-520x448.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="448" /></a>

2.你可以在網頁左邊找到「Personal Document Settings」，點進去，然後在「Approved Personal Document E-mail List」這邊新增一組信箱：kindle@klip.me

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_124856.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-5966" title="2012-01-24_124856" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_124856-520x481.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="481" /></a>

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_124916.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-5967" title="2012-01-24_124916" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_124916-520x289.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="289" /></a>

3.接下來你可以在同一個網頁上方「Send-to-Kindle E-Mail Settings」那邊找到你的Kindle傳送檔案用的信箱，把它複製起來

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_125149.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-5968" title="2012-01-24_125149" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_125149-520x488.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="488" /></a>

4.對Send to Kindle按右鍵->「選項」，然後在「My Kindle's email address:」那邊輸入你剛剛記下的信箱，只需要輸入＠之前的文字

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_125405.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-5969" title="2012-01-24_125405" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_125405-520x488.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="488" /></a>

5.設定好之後，按一下「Save」即可儲存設定（網頁要往下拉一點）

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_125609.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-5970" title="2012-01-24_125609" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_125609-520x488.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="488" /></a>

6.接下來，打開你想要傳送到Kindle的網頁，然後按一下Send to Kindle的按鈕，它會自動幫你把不必要的元素去除掉，然後你所看到的畫面，就是它會傳送到Kindle裡面的資料

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_125914.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-5971" title="2012-01-24_125914" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_125914-520x416.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="416" /></a>

另外，在這個畫面你可以去做一些編修，增加一些文字，直接點選你要修改的部份即可，把它當成一個簡易版的Word而不要當他是網頁

7.按下Send，它就會傳送過去囉，一般經驗要等10分鐘左右才會在你的Kindle裡面出現

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_130145.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-5972" title="2012-01-24_130145" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-24_130145-520x416.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="416" /></a>

另外要注意，在某些情況下，傳送檔案到Kindle是要收費的，詳細情形可以參閱：<a href="http://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=200505520&amp;#fees">http://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=200505520&amp;#fees</a>
