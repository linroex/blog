Today I want to introduce a plugin called Subscribe To Comments. It can automatically tell commenters that their comment has been replied to, so they don't have to come back and check for themselves. Subscribe To Comments was recommended to me by Brother Sausage, and since I think it's pretty useful, I'm sharing it with everyone.

<h2>Subscribe To Comments Tutorial:</h2>
1. Upload the subscribe-to-comments main program and the Chinese localization file to /wp-content/plugins

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/06/2009-06-20-3.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1410" title="2009-06-20-3" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/06/2009-06-20-3.jpg" alt="2009-06-20-3" width="450" height="267" /></a>

2. Go into the WP admin backend, and click Plugins - Installed (already installed) in order

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/06/2009-06-20_2052251.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1409" title="2009-06-20_2052251" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/06/2009-06-20_2052251.jpg" alt="2009-06-20_2052251" width="362" height="458" /></a>

3. Scroll the browser down to the "Inactive Plugins" section, then click "Activate" after subscribe-to-comments<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/06/2009-06-20-4.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1411" title="2009-06-20-4" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/06/2009-06-20-4.jpg" alt="2009-06-20-4" width="450" height="179" /></a>

At this point the plugin installation is done. Next, you can click the

"subscribe to comments" option under Settings to start configuring the various parameters

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/06/2009-06-20-5.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1412" title="2009-06-20-5" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/06/2009-06-20-5.jpg" alt="2009-06-20-5" width="176" height="337" /></a>

Here you can set the name and email, etc. that you want to show when emails are sent out

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/06/2009-06-20-6.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1413" title="2009-06-20-6" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/06/2009-06-20-6.jpg" alt="2009-06-20-6" width="500" height="360" /></a>

That's it! From now on, as soon as you reply to someone's comment, they'll receive a notification
