I'm sure you've often seen a guestbook plugin in the sidebar of other people's blogs and wanted one for yourself, right? Today I'm introducing Cbox, one of the best free guestbook services on the web. Cbox is excellent in many ways, but the maximum number of comments is 100, so you have to delete old comments regularly. Its anti-spam is also weak, so it's easy for advertising comments to flood it. = = Even so, Cbox has some advantages, such as letting you use emoticons. Below, I'll show you how to sign up for Cbox.

URL: <a href="http://www.cbox.ws/">http://www.cbox.ws/</a>

1. Go to the Cbox homepage and click Sign up.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/06/2009-06-10_194731.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1274" title="2009-06-10_194731" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/06/2009-06-10_194731.jpg" alt="2009-06-10_194731" width="640" height="147" /></a>


<!--more-->


2. Fill in the required information (if you can't read English, see the explanations below).

<span style="color: #0000ff;">Cbox name</span> is the account name you want.

<span style="color: #0000ff;">Email address</span> is your email address.

<span style="color: #0000ff;">Password</span> is the password you want.

<span style="color: #0000ff;">Confirm Password</span> is where you enter your password again.

<span style="color: #0000ff;">Website</span> is your website URL (your blog's address).

<span style="color: #0000ff;">Language</span> is the language you use. Choose "Chinese(trad)".

<span style="color: #0000ff;">Style</span> is the look you want (the default is fine).

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/06/2009-06-10_195149.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1275" title="2009-06-10_195149" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/06/2009-06-10_195149.jpg" alt="2009-06-10_195149" width="533" height="298" /></a>

After filling in your information, check the box next to "I have read and agree to the Cbox <a href="javascript:popwin('terms', 'terms_pop.php', 500, 400);">terms and conditions</a> of service." Then click the "Create my Cbox!" button to finish signing up.

If the sign-up succeeds, they'll send an email to your inbox.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/06/2009-06-10_200713.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1276" title="2009-06-10_200713" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/06/2009-06-10_200713.jpg" alt="2009-06-10_200713" width="640" height="461" /></a>

Click the link in the email to complete verification.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/06/2009-06-10_201427.jpg">
</a>

Once verification is done, of course, the next step is to log in! Enter your account name and password in the upper right, then click the arrow to log in.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/06/2009-06-10_201718.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1278" title="2009-06-10_201718" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/06/2009-06-10_201718.jpg" alt="2009-06-10_201718" width="470" height="36" /></a>

After logging in, you'll see a page called Code Generator.

There's a field labeled "<strong>Step 1: </strong> Get your code". Copy the code in it and paste it into your blog.
