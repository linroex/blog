When you buy a new computer, I think the most troublesome part is choosing antivirus software. For ordinary people, a computer without antivirus is genuinely dangerous. But many antivirus programs cost money, pirated copies are unsafe, and free ones might not be strong enough and leave room for viruses and trojans. Here I want to introduce a "free" antivirus called Avast. It's one of the best free antivirus programs out there, and in my opinion, it's number one! It even beats some paid antivirus programs. So if you want to save the money you'd spend on antivirus software, give Avast a try.


<!--more-->
Avast is free, but its features are no less than those of paid antivirus programs. It has basic scanning, of course, and you can schedule scans or run a scan at startup. It also has a <strong>"Sandbox"</strong> feature, which usually exists only in paid antivirus programs. A sandbox lets you designate a program to open inside the sandbox. Whatever changes that sandboxed program makes are reverted after a reboot. So if you open a virus in the sandbox, whatever it does to your computer will basically be undone once you restart. Of course, a sandbox can still have holes that someone finds and breaks, so don't rely on it too much XD Avast also offers <strong>"Site Blocking"</strong>, which lets you specify URLs to block. This is useful for keeping kids at home away from adult websites... or from spending too much time on Facebook...

Last but not least is Avast's <strong>"Quiet Mode"</strong>. I'm sure many people have had the experience of being in the middle of a game when an antivirus alert pulls them back to the desktop. If it's just a casual game, it's no big deal, but sometimes it happens in the middle of a boss fight! By the time you return to the game window, your character is already dead... For this reason, many antivirus programs have faced the threat of being turned off... But now Avast has a built-in Quiet Mode. You can enable it in the settings, and then no warnings or notifications will pop up. You can also set it to suppress messages while a full-screen program is running, so you can avoid the regret of being killed by the boss.

<h2>Setting Up Site Blocking</h2>
This feature is mainly designed for users with children at home. You can turn it on and block access to certain sites. For example, if a parent sees their child spending too much time on Facebook, they can block the Facebook URL to keep the child from getting hooked XD

1. Go to "Other Protection" → "Site Blocking" and check "Enable Site Blocking"
