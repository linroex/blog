WinRAR是目前市場上佔有率最高的壓縮軟體,WinRAR與7-zip一樣都有支援許多的功能,除了最基本的壓縮.解壓縮以外,還有分片檔案分割、資料恢復、資料加密等功能,另外WinRAR還可以讓你做出自解壓縮檔,不需要安裝解壓縮程式就可以開啟檔案,十分方便,只不過WinRAR是一套商業性的軟體,你只能試用40天,過了40天你就必需要付費。

<h3>一.WinRAR安裝教學</h3>

<h3>二.WinRAR壓縮檔案教學</h3>
1.對要被壓縮的檔案按右鍵,然後選擇【WinRAR】→【加到壓縮檔】

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/20091107_164141.jpg"><img style="border-right-width: 0px; display: inline; border-top-width: 0px; border-bottom-width: 0px; border-left-width: 0px" title="2009-11-07_164141" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/20091107_164141_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="2009-11-07_164141" width="512" height="484" /></a>

2.在壓縮檔名稱那裡設定壓縮檔要存放的位置(按瀏覽),然後設定壓縮檔的格式(RAR或ZIP)

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/20091107_164420.jpg"><img style="border-right-width: 0px; display: inline; border-top-width: 0px; border-bottom-width: 0px; border-left-width: 0px" title="2009-11-07_164420" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/20091107_164420_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="2009-11-07_164420" width="417" height="370" /></a>

3.設定壓縮方式,看你是要加快壓縮速度或是好的壓縮率都可以選擇

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/20091107_164557.jpg"><img style="border-right-width: 0px; display: inline; border-top-width: 0px; border-bottom-width: 0px; border-left-width: 0px" title="2009-11-07_164557" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/20091107_164557_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="2009-11-07_164557" width="417" height="370" /></a>

4.如果要設密碼的話,依序點選【進階設定】→【設定密碼】,然後輸入你要的密碼在按【確定】即可

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/20091107_164810.jpg"><img style="border-right-width: 0px; display: inline; border-top-width: 0px; border-bottom-width: 0px; border-left-width: 0px" title="2009-11-07_164810" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/20091107_164810_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="2009-11-07_164810" width="419" height="374" /></a>

5.接下來按確定就會開始壓縮了

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/20091107_164920.jpg"><img style="border-right-width: 0px; display: inline; border-top-width: 0px; border-bottom-width: 0px; border-left-width: 0px" title="2009-11-07_164920" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/20091107_164920_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="2009-11-07_164920" width="417" height="370" /></a>

6.正在壓縮檔案中

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/20091107_164949.jpg"><img style="border-right-width: 0px; border-top-width: 0px; border-bottom-width: 0px; border-left-width: 0px" title="2009-11-07_164949" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/20091107_164949_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="2009-11-07_164949" width="313" height="260" /></a>

另外如果你不想設定這麼麻煩的話,你也可以直接按【WinRAR】→【加到XXX.rar】

<h3>三.WinRAR解壓縮教學</h3>
1.對要解壓縮的檔案點右鍵,依序點選【WinRAR】→【解壓縮檔案至此】

P.S.必須是對已經壓縮過的檔案點右鍵才會出現【解壓縮檔案至此】

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/20091107_165442.jpg"><img style="border-right-width: 0px; display: inline; border-top-width: 0px; border-bottom-width: 0px; border-left-width: 0px" title="2009-11-07_165442" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/20091107_165442_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="2009-11-07_165442" width="415" height="407" /></a>

2.接下來WinRAR就會開始解壓縮檔案,不過如果檔案有設密碼的話會跳出一個視窗要求你輸入密碼,就輸入給他吧~不然暴力破解可是要很久滴^^

3.正在解壓縮檔案,看你檔案的大小會決定解壓縮的時間
