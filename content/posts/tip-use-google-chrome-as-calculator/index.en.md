Before I start, I should say that I don't know whether other people already know about this trick. I just stumbled onto it, so I'm sharing it here. If you already know it, feel free to skip this post. And please don't leave comments calling it a rehash!

I discovered this while doing some calculations. Sometimes you need to work with big numbers, and you reach for a calculator. But most calculators require pressing buttons, which is fine for short sums. For a long expression like 1489*6875*478, though, you'd be pressing buttons for a long time, right?
<!--more-->
Actually, Google Chrome has a built-in calculator. Just type your expression into the address bar and it will give you the answer!

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/02/2010-02-12_132155.png"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2675" title="2010-02-12_132155" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/02/2010-02-12_132155-500x154.png" alt="" width="500" height="154" /></a>

Here are some common operators you need to use in Google Chrome. For example, use ^ for exponents and * for multiplication.

Chrome also supports brackets of every size: curly braces {}, square brackets [], and parentheses ().

For example: (125+759)*2
