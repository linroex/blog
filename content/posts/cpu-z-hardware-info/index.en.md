<h2>CPU-Z<span style="font-weight: normal; font-size: 13px;">I think most people have heard of it. It is a small program that quickly shows you information about your computer. CPU-Z tells you basic details such as your CPU model, your memory size, and your motherboard's manufacturer and model. It even shows the CPU's manufacturing process clearly. You can check all kinds of hardware details without opening the case. CPU-Z can also export the data to txt or htm files.</span></h2>
