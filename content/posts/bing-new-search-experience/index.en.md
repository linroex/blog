<a href="http://www.bing.com/">Bing</a> sounds, in Chinese, like the first part of the word for "colorful" (bīnfēn), so I think Microsoft probably hopes its search can be "colorful" too! I tried it today and honestly I was speechless. I suspect it's because it's still in beta. Its language search only offers 3 restrictions, and its category search has only 3 categories: web, news, and images................ It really can't compare to Google. Its entry page looks very nice, but once you get into the search results page, everything is white = = It's no different from Google either. And its search ability is actually the same as the old MSN search engine, so personally I think this will be a major failure for MSN.


<!--more-->


Even though Bing's features get criticized as worthless, I still think it has some strengths! For example, it can find things that many guys love the most: adult videos, and you can even play them online right away. Pretty great, right? Of course, as the site owner, I never watch those~~ But Bing really goes a bit overboard! Its image search even lets you set strange positions such as face, head, and shoulders. This may be the only area where it can compete with Google!
