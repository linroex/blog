還記得上次本站有做過一次投票吧!那次使用的投票服務就是由PollDaddy所提供的,個人蠻喜歡用PollDaddy的線上投票服務,雖然介面是英文的,但是它本身的投票器卻可以輸入中文而不會出現亂碼,還有他還是一個免費的服務,也不像有些網站會放廣告到投票機上面,PollDaddy是不會有這種情況發生滴~~

PollDaddy特色:
<ul>
	<li>免費</li>
	<li>沒有廣告</li>
	<li>需申請會員</li>
	<li>支援中文,無亂碼問題</li>
	<li>投票機內建多種佈景</li>
</ul>
關鍵字:PollDaddy,線上投票,免費投票,部落格外掛

網站網址 <a href="http://www.polldaddy.com/">http://www.polldaddy.com/</a>
<h2>一.帳號申請</h2>
1.先開啟PollDaddy的網站,然後點選SignUp

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_101150.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1570" title="2009-07-09_101150" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_101150.jpg" alt="2009-07-09_101150" width="500" height="174" /></a>

2.進入到Signup以後,他會提供你3種選擇,第一種是免費的,第2.3都是付費的,基本上使用免費的就可以了,值得一提的是免費會員每個月的統計量最高到100人,還有每個問題最多只能放10個答案(這夠用了啦!!)

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_101554.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1571" title="2009-07-09_101554" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_101554.jpg" alt="2009-07-09_101554" width="499" height="239" /></a>

3.現在要開始填寫申請資料了,看不懂英文的去開Google翻譯吧~ 開玩笑的啦,這裡就提供你翻譯,不用再跑去Google了XD

<span style="color: #0000ff;">First name 你的英文名字</span>
<div>

<span style="color: #0000ff;">Last name 同First name</span>

<span style="color: #0000ff;">Email address 電子信箱地址</span>

<span style="color: #0000ff;">Re-enter email address 確認電子信箱地址</span>

<span style="color: #0000ff;">Password 密碼</span>

<span style="color: #0000ff;">Re-enter password 確認密碼</span>

最後你把"<label style="color: #333333;" for="uTerms">Please indicate that you agree to our <a style="color: #333333;" href="http://www.polldaddy.com/terms/"><strong>terms &amp; conditions</strong></a>.</label>"那項打勾,然後再"<label for="recaptcha_response_field"><span id="recaptcha_instructions_image">Type the two words:</span><span id="recaptcha_instructions_audio">Type what you hear:</span><span id="recaptcha_instructions_error">Incorrect. Try again."</span></label>下面輸入圖片中的驗證碼在按Signup就完成申請了

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_102158.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1572" title="2009-07-09_102158" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_102158.jpg" alt="2009-07-09_102158" width="500" height="303" /></a>

4.申請完帳號以後你可以到你信箱去收信囉

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_102352.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1573" title="2009-07-09_102352" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_102352.jpg" alt="2009-07-09_102352" width="615" height="342" /></a>
<h2>二.投票服務建立</h2>
申請完帳號以後當然要建立投票器了,第一步一樣是進入到PollDaddy的官網

然後點選Login並輸入帳號跟密碼(帳號=申請時的信箱)

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_102723.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1574" title="2009-07-09_102723" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_102723.jpg" alt="2009-07-09_102723" width="599" height="432" /></a>

2.登錄以後你可以在左邊看到你已經建立的投票,大家都是剛申請所以沒有東西,我是因為很早就辦了也有用過XD

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_102914.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1575" title="2009-07-09_102914" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_102914.jpg" alt="2009-07-09_102914" width="500" height="360" /></a>

不過我們的重點是在建立新的投票機,所以請按一下Create A New Poll

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_103006.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1576" title="2009-07-09_103006" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_103006.jpg" alt="2009-07-09_103006" width="408" height="248" /></a>

3.按Create A New Poll進入到增加投票機的頁面以後,在<span style="color: #0000ff;">"Please enter your poll question here"</span>這欄輸入投票機的標題(問題)

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_104902.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1577" title="2009-07-09_104902" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_104902.jpg" alt="2009-07-09_104902" width="499" height="300" /></a>

4.在"Enter your answers here"這欄設定你的答案

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_105213.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1578" title="2009-07-09_105213" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_105213.jpg" alt="2009-07-09_105213" width="500" height="310" /></a>

如果5個預設的答案不夠用可以按Add a new answer

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_105321.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1579" title="2009-07-09_105321" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_105321.jpg" alt="2009-07-09_105321" width="500" height="309" /></a>

5.在Select a style for your poll那欄選擇你要用的樣式(共有19款可用)

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_105956.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1580" title="2009-07-09_105956" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_105956.jpg" alt="2009-07-09_105956" width="500" height="293" /></a>

6.全部都設定完以後你可以在設定其他的東西(右邊那裡),基本上我都是維持預設值

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_110339.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1581" title="2009-07-09_110339" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_110339.jpg" alt="2009-07-09_110339" width="244" height="537" /></a>

7.最後按一下那個Save............的按鈕來儲存設定

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_110420.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1582" title="2009-07-09_110420" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-09_110420.jpg" alt="2009-07-09_110420" width="415" height="338" /></a>
