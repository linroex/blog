While browsing the web today, I was surprised to find that I couldn't connect to Microsoft's website. It reminded me of the disaster when my computer broke down last time.... and sure enough, it really happened again!

Last time, for some unknown reason, my computer couldn't connect to any Microsoft-related sites, nor could it open antivirus software websites, and the virus definitions wouldn't update either. Back then I just reinstalled everything directly, since the computer had been used for quite a while. But this time the computer was barely bought and it's already acting up @@

After all that rambling, the reason I'm recommending FixDwndp today is this: I personally suggest that readers of this article go check right away whether your computer has the following symptoms:
<ol>
	<li>You can't get into the Microsoft official website</li>
	<li>You can't get into the websites of major antivirus software</li>
	<li>Automatic update notifications haven't popped up for a long time</li>
	<li>Antivirus software can't update its virus definitions

</li>
</ol>
