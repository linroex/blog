<strong></strong><strong>e</strong><strong>buddy</strong> is a web-based instant messaging site that I've found quite good so far. It supports many different instant messaging services, including well-known ones like Yahoo Messenger, MSN, GTalk, and QQ (which is popular in mainland China). <strong>ebuddy </strong>also supports Facebook, though I don't really understand what Facebook has to do with instant messaging = =. Besides supporting many IM services, <strong>ebuddy</strong> also offers many languages, including Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, and English, so you don't have to worry about not understanding the interface. But what I like best about <strong>ebuddy</strong> is that it lets you sign up for one account and manage all your different IM accounts together. That way, you only need to remember one account, which is like remembering many accounts ^^

This article will introduce how to log in to <strong>ebuddy</strong>, change the language, and use your ebuddy ID.


<!--more-->

<h2>ebuddy Features</h2>
<ul>
	<li>Supports Yahoo, MSN, GTalk, and QQ</li>
	<li>Works without installing Flash</li>
	<li>You can sign up for an ebuddy account to manage your various IM accounts</li>
	<li>Supports multiple languages (including Traditional Chinese)</li>
	<li>Multi-person chat conferences</li>
	<li>Video chat supported</li>
</ul>
Website: <a href="http://www.ebuddy.com/">http://www.ebuddy.com/</a>
<h2>Changing the Interface Language to Traditional Chinese</h2>
Since <a href="http://www.ebuddy.com/">ebuddy</a>'s built-in language is Simplified Chinese, when you first visit the <a href="http://www.ebuddy.com/">ebuddy</a> website, click "Site Languages" in the upper right corner and select Traditional Chinese to switch the page to Traditional characters~

<h2>Logging In to ebuddy</h2>
In this section, we'll show you how to log in to various instant messaging services. However, we won't explain how to use each IM service itself, because <a href="http://www.ebuddy.com/">ebuddy</a> supports so many IM services that covering them all would take us years!

1.First, click the instant messaging service you want to use

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/2009-10-25-10-46-33.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-2093" title="2009-10-25 10 46 33" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/2009-10-25-10-46-33.jpg" alt="2009-10-25 10 46 33" width="498" height="359" /></a>

2.Enter your account and password, then click Log in

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/2009-10-25-10-48-32.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-2094" title="2009-10-25 10 48 32" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/2009-10-25-10-48-32.jpg" alt="2009-10-25 10 48 32" width="504" height="268" /></a>

3.A pop-up window will open next. If your browser has a pop-up blocker installed, turn it off first or add <a href="http://www.ebuddy.com/">ebuddy</a> to its whitelist.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/2009-10-25-10-51-19.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-2095" title="2009-10-25 10 51 19" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/2009-10-25-10-51-19.jpg" alt="2009-10-25 10 51 19" width="520" height="487" /></a>

4.Now you can start chatting with other people. I won't explain how to chat, so feel free to explore on your own. If you have any questions, leave a comment and ask me.

<h2><span style="color: #ffffff;"><span style="color: #000000;">Applying for an ebuddy Account</span></span></h2>
As I mentioned at the beginning of this article, ebuddy lets you sign up for an account with them to manage your other IM accounts in one place. This is very convenient for people who use many IM services, so in this section, we'll show you how to use ebuddy to manage your accounts. However, since your ebuddy ID holds all of your IM accounts, be very careful not to let anyone know your password!

Note: By "managing," I mean that "you only need to enter your ebuddy ID on the ebuddy website, and it will log you in to each IM service automatically."

1.Click [ebuddy ID] in the left menu, then click [<span style="color: #ff3300;">Apply for your free, personal eBuddy ID</span>]

<h2>Using Your ebuddy ID</h2>
In the previous article, we signed up for and set up an ebuddy ID. In this one, we'll show you how to log in to all the IM services you've set up.

1.As usual, first log in to ebuddy, then select [Settings] → [Accounts], and click Log in on the IM service you want to use. If you find clicking each one tedious, you can also click [Log in all], then click OK to return to the main screen.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/2009-10-25-11-43-00.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-2106" title="2009-10-25 11 43 00" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/2009-10-25-11-43-00.jpg" alt="2009-10-25 11 43 00" width="520" height="487" /></a>

2.Once you're back on the main screen, you'll notice that your contact list is several times longer than before. That's because you've logged in to all of your IM accounts. This feature is really convenient, isn't it!!

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/2009-10-25-11-46-59.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-2107" title="2009-10-25 11 46 59" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/2009-10-25-11-46-59.jpg" alt="2009-10-25 11 46 59" width="520" height="487" /></a>

3.Unfortunately, ebuddy doesn't show names, only the other person's account name, so it's a bit tiring to recognize people = =
