<strong>TeamViewer</strong> is a very convenient remote control program. Its biggest feature is that it can get past firewall restrictions, so you can still use it even if your computer has a firewall. I think <strong>TeamViewer</strong> probably works by first connecting to its server and then connecting to the controlled computer, but that would be quite costly. In addition, <strong>TeamViewer</strong> offers a special feature: you can customize a "TeamViewer of your own." This is a service provided on the official website, and it lets you replace the logo with your own. Amazing, right!

<h3>2. TeamViewer Versions</h3>
Full version: As the name suggests, this is the version that supports all of TeamViewer's features. It is more troublesome to configure, but it lets you control the connection speed and set more detailed options, such as whether to keep the desktop during a connection.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/09/2009-09-20-003.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1934" title="2009-09-20-003" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/09/2009-09-20-003.jpg" alt="2009-09-20-003" width="550" height="381" /></a>

Simple version: A program that only has remote control functions. No adjustment is needed (and none is possible). Also, the simple version cannot control other people's computers; it can only be controlled by others.

<h3>3. Making a Personalized TeamViewer</h3>
A personalized TeamViewer means you can put your own logo on TeamViewer. However, this only works for TeamViewer QuickSupport (the version that others can connect to); the other versions do not support it, and the method does not work for them either. Just follow the tutorial below.

1. Prepare an image of 200*100 pixels (it can be JPEG, BMP, or GIF). Then go to the <a href="http://www.teamviewer.com/download/index.aspx">download</a> page of TeamViewer. Scroll down a little and you will see the line "<em>For the instant customer:</em> TeamViewer QuickSupport." Click <a title="Download Windows" href="http://www.teamviewer.com/download/customdesign_4x.aspx">Customize Quicksupport</a> under this line.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/09/2009-09-26_184545.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1947" title="2009-09-26_184545" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/09/2009-09-26_184545.jpg" alt="2009-09-26_184545" width="549" height="212" /></a>

2. On the settings page of the personalized TeamViewer, fill in the relevant information, then upload the image you prepared earlier.

Explanation of the labels:
<ul>
	<li>Language: keep the default value.</li>
	<li><span id="customDesign_titleDescription">Title: </span>The title of TeamViewer, shown at the top of the program</li>
	<li><span id="customDesign_explanationDescription">Text: appears in the personalized </span>TeamViewer content</li>
	<li>Logo: upload your logo.</li>
</ul>
<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/09/2009-09-26_185013.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1948" title="2009-09-26_185013" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/09/2009-09-26_185013.jpg" alt="2009-09-26_185013" width="321" height="379" /></a>

<span id="customDesign_lblTextColor">Text color: sets the text color. Click the box to bring up the color picker.</span>

<span id="customDesign_lblBGColor">Background color: sets the background color. The method is about the same.</span>

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/09/2009-09-26_185206.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1949" title="2009-09-26_185206" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/09/2009-09-26_185206.jpg" alt="2009-09-26_185206" width="675" height="312" /></a>

Leave the rest of the settings as they are, then click "<span id="customDesign_btnLabel">Generate QuickSupport</span>."

3. Click the button inside the red box to download it.
