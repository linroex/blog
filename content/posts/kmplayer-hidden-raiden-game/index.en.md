I think many people have played the shooter Raiden, so I'll give a quick introduction here. Raiden is a shooter centered on airplanes. The theme is an alien invasion, and a space fighter called Raiden goes out to fight them. You may not have known it was called Raiden while playing, because even I didn't know that when I played it xD. Raiden has come out in many versions. It's on the iPhone (iPod Touch), on the PSP (I think....), and on mobile phones too, so you can find it almost everywhere. Raiden really is a classic game.


<!--more-->


Raiden's introduction: <a href="http://goo.gl/vIf1 ">http://goo.gl/vIf1</a>

Confused by the title? KMPlayer is a media player, so why would a classic shooter come up? I saw this on the Yili Forum just now. I thought it was strange too (I assumed the moderator was bluffing ^^). But after I opened KMPlayer myself, I found the moderator wasn't bluffing, and Raiden really can be launched from it. I guess the original author of KMPlayer must have loved Raiden, so they added Raiden into KMPlayer.
<h2>Opening the Hidden Raiden in KMPlayer</h2>
1.Open KMPlayer

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-17_225147.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3297" title="2010-06-17_225147" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-17_225147-550x343.jpg" alt="" width="550" height="343" /></a>

2.Right-click in the video playback area and choose 【Options】→【About】

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-17_225910.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3298" title="2010-06-17_225910" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-17_225910-550x386.jpg" alt="" width="550" height="386" /></a>

3.Double-click the KMPlayer logo with the mouse. Wait a moment and the game will load.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-17_230216.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3299" title="2010-06-17_230216" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-17_230216.jpg" alt="" width="512" height="512" /></a>

4.After the game loads, click START with the mouse to begin.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-17_230352.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3300" title="2010-06-17_230352" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-17_230352-550x364.jpg" alt="" width="550" height="364" /></a>

5.Press Enter on the keyboard on NEW GAME, and you can start playing Raiden~

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-17_230619.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3301" title="2010-06-17_230619" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-17_230619-550x364.jpg" alt="" width="550" height="364" /></a>

6.The arrow keys control movement up, down, left, and right. The Z key shoots, and the X key drops a bomb (a wide-area attack). From my own trial play, it's hard to control @@. If you get hit by accident, you die, so the system gives you many lives. Honestly, I'd rather it had a health bar system.
