<span style="font-weight: normal; font-size: 13px;">You often see people recommend Android apps online without giving a URL, just a barcode to scan for the download. That barcode is actually a QR Code, and a decoder app on Android can read the download link stored inside it. Apple Daily's Animated News app also offers a square barcode like this. It's also a QR Code, so you need special software to decode it into a URL.</span>


<!--more-->

There are many apps on Android that can decode QR Codes, but the Barcode Scanner I'm introducing today works on most phone resolutions (if it works on my oddball IDEOS resolution, it should work on other phones...). <span style="font-weight: normal; font-size: 13px;">Barcode Scanner</span><span style="font-weight: normal; font-size: 13px;"> is also the most popular barcode scanner. It supports batch scanning too, so you can keep scanning barcodes without recording each URL after every scan. The app saves the barcodes automatically.</span>

<h2>How to Use Barcode Scanner</h2>
1. Search for Barcode Scanner in the Android Market and install it.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/snap20110130_160133.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-4556" title="snap20110130_160133" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/snap20110130_160133.jpg" alt="" width="240" height="320" /></a>

2. Open Barcode Scanner.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/snap20110130_160332.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-4557" title="snap20110130_160332" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/snap20110130_160332.jpg" alt="" width="240" height="320" /></a>

3. The first time you use it, you'll see this welcome screen. Tap Finish to skip it.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/snap20110130_160346.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-4558" title="snap20110130_160346" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/snap20110130_160346.jpg" alt="" width="240" height="320" /></a>

4. Point the camera lens at the barcode. It scans automatically, so you don't need to press any buttons.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/snap20110130_160355.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-4559" title="snap20110130_160355" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/snap20110130_160355.jpg" alt="" width="240" height="320" /></a>

5. After a successful scan, this window appears. You can choose to open the URL in the browser or share it with others. If you want to install an Android app, choose Open Browser.
