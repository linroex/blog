You've probably seen a scrolling widget on other people's blogs. That's actually a service from blogupp. blogupp can get your posts seen by more people, but your blog needs to have an RSS feed (by the way, my RSS has been having problems = =).

Some people may wonder how blogupp works. Basically, blogupp just reads your blog's RSS feed. It's a bit like a blog syndication network, but blogupp's reach is even bigger. blogupp also supports multiple languages. In fact, as soon as you enter the blogupp website, it automatically switches to Chinese!

Website: <a href="http://www.blogupp.com/">http://www.blogupp.com/</a>
<h2>blogupp Features</h2>
<ul>
	<li>No account registration required</li>
	<li>Your email and privacy are never exposed</li>
	<li>No extra advertising agreements or other requirements</li>
	<li>Anti-fraud system and fair sharing principles</li>
	<li>Reach your target readers</li>
	<li>Exposure rewards for new users</li>
	<li>Good support for Blogger, WordPress, and other similar platforms</li>
	<li>The industry's highest exchange ratio (10:9)</li>
</ul>
<h2>
<!--more-->
<span style="color: #ff0000;">How to Use:</span></h2>
1. Connect to blogupp

2. In the field labeled <strong>"1. </strong>Enter your blog address", enter your blog URL and click Submit.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/2009-10-25_212500.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-2110" title="2009-10-25_212500" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/2009-10-25_212500.jpg" alt="2009-10-25_212500" width="491" height="352" /></a>

3. Choose the layout you want. There are three options: top and bottom bars (like this site's), a single column, and left and right columns. If you can't see them clearly, click the small image and an example will appear on the right.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/2009-10-25_212904.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-2112" title="2009-10-25_212904" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/2009-10-25_212904.jpg" alt="2009-10-25_212904" width="491" height="352" /></a>

4. Copy the code, then paste it onto your blog.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/2009-10-25_213052.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-2113" title="2009-10-25_213052" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/2009-10-25_213052.jpg" alt="2009-10-25_213052" width="491" height="352" /></a>

5. If your blog doesn't support iframe code, click the link "Your blog doesn't support this code? Try another one" and blogupp will show you a different code.
