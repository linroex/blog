<strong><a href="http://tools.google.com/dlpage/cloudconnect">Google Cloud Connect</a></strong> is a small add-on for Microsoft Office designed by Google. It connects Microsoft Office to Google Docs, so you can save your documents to Google Docs in the cloud and collaborate online directly from Microsoft Office. The unfortunate part is that it doesn't let you edit Google Docs documents directly inside Microsoft Office. It would be great if you could. To be honest, Google Docs is very handy, but it crashes quite often, and it has far too few features...


<!--more-->

<h2>Google Cloud Connect features</h2>
<ul>
	<li>Gives Office online collaboration, cloud backup, and version restore features</li>
</ul>

<h2>Google Cloud Connect usage guide</h2>
1. First, open Word, and you'll see a new item in it. (Google Cloud Connect actually installs itself into Word, Excel, and PowerPoint. For convenience, I'll use Word as the example.)

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/2011-03-19_195237.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-4935" title="2011-03-19_195237" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/2011-03-19_195237-520x311.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="311" /></a>

2. Click the Google Cloud Connect button on the left, then select "Google Cloud Connect Setting"

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/2011-03-19_195403.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-4937" title="2011-03-19_195403" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/2011-03-19_195403-520x311.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="311" /></a>

3. Click "Login" to sign in with your Google account

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/2011-03-19_195529.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-4938" title="2011-03-19_195529" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/2011-03-19_195529-520x274.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="274" /></a>

4. Enter your Google account email and password, then click "Sign in"

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/2011-03-19_195624.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-4939" title="2011-03-19_195624" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/2011-03-19_195624-448x500.jpg" alt="" width="448" height="500" /></a>

5. Click "Grant access"

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/2011-03-19_195732.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-4940" title="2011-03-19_195732" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/2011-03-19_195732-448x500.jpg" alt="" width="448" height="500" /></a>

6. Once you've completed the steps above, you're connected to Google Docs. Next, choose how to sync: automatically, or only when you click the Sync button.
<ul>
	<li>Automatically-&gt;Automatic sync</li>
	<li>Manually-&gt;Manual sync</li>
</ul>
<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/2011-03-19_200144.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-4941" title="2011-03-19_200144" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/2011-03-19_200144-520x358.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="358" /></a>

7. After setting it up, you can click Sync to synchronize
