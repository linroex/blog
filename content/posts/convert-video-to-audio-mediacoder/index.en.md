This post shows you how to turn a "video" into "music." Often you might download a great music video from a video-sharing site (for example, YouTube), but the file is in a video format such as MP4 or FLV. In that case, you can use the audio track extraction feature built into MediaCoder to convert the video into an audio file!


<!--more-->


For the basics and installation of MediaCoder, see my earlier post <a href="https://coder.tw/?p=5722" target="_blank">"MediaCoder: A Free Transcoder with GPGPU Support"</a>
<h2>Audio Track Extraction Tutorial</h2>
1. Launch MediaCoder, load the video you want to process, go to the "Audio" tab, and choose "MP3" in the "Format" field.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-26_160556.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-6016" title="2012-01-26_160556" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-26_160556-520x488.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="488" /></a>

2. Set the "Output folder," then go to "Job" -> "Transcode audio only."

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-26_160850.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-6017" title="2012-01-26_160850" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/2012-01-26_160850-520x287.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="287" /></a>

3. Done!

<strong>Summary:</strong> MediaCoder is really convenient. It has audio track extraction built in, and it works well with <a href="https://coder.tw/?p=6008" target="_blank">FastestTube</a>, which I introduced earlier. Download the videos from YouTube with FastestTube, then use this audio track extraction feature to batch-convert them into music!
