Facesaerch is a search engine where you type in a keyword and it finds pictures for you. It uses Google's API to find images, which I personally think is pretty amazing. However, it doesn't seem to support Chinese searches, and when I typed in Chinese, it was hard to find anything. Its accuracy isn't great either = = (the more famous a person is, the easier they are to find). But I just tried it, and when I typed in my nickname, linroex, it actually found my photos ㄟ :)

How to use it: first, go to the Facesaerch website, enter your keyword in the red box, then click Search to start the search.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/2009-05-31_101454.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1169" title="2009-05-31_101454" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/2009-05-31_101454.jpg" alt="2009-05-31_101454" width="640" height="461" /></a>

Once the images are found, use your mouse wheel to scroll through them to find the one you want (if there are a lot of images), or drag the horizontal bar at the bottom with your mouse.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/2009-05-31_101629.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1170" title="2009-05-31_101629" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/2009-05-31_101629.jpg" alt="2009-05-31_101629" width="1024" height="738" /></a>

If you want to know where a picture came from, just double-click the image and its source website will pop up!!

Personally, I think the chances of needing this search engine are very low. The only time I used it was for a school report! It's pretty handy for finding pictures, though. Also, it only supports face searches. I once tried entering a place name or something else, and I couldn't find any results!
