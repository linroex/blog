<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/2011-08-07_165914.jpg"><img class="size-full wp-image-5593 aligncenter" title="2011-08-07_165914" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/2011-08-07_165914.jpg" alt="" width="307" height="230" /></a><a href="http://doominow.com/" target="_blank">Doomi</a> is an application built on Adobe AIR technology that helps you create a ToDoList, which is basically a memo list. There are many programs similar to Doomi. For example, Google Desktop has widgets with this kind of function, and even iOS 5 plans to include a built-in ToDoList app called Reminders. What makes Doomi stand out is its intuitive, good-looking interface. You hardly need any tutorial; as soon as you see the interface, you know how to use it. Also, you need to install Adobe AIR before you can use Doomi.

<h2>Doomi Tutorial</h2>
<div>1.First, install Adobe AIR, then go to Doomi's <a href="http://doominow.com/" target="_blank">official website</a> and click "Install"</div>
<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/2011-08-07_170159.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-5594" title="2011-08-07_170159" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/2011-08-07_170159-520x357.jpg" alt="" width="520" height="357" /></a>

2.A window will pop up asking whether you want to open or save the file. Click "Open."

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/2011-08-07_170236.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-5595" title="2011-08-07_170236" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/2011-08-07_170236.jpg" alt="" width="486" height="228" /></a>

3.This screen shows information about the program you're about to install. Click "Install."

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/2011-08-07_170703.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-5596" title="2011-08-07_170703" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/2011-08-07_170703.jpg" alt="" width="486" height="393" /></a>

4.Next, it asks where you want to install Doomi. By default it's usually on the C drive, but you can change it to another location. Click "Continue" and the installation will begin!

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/2011-08-07_170748.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-5597" title="2011-08-07_170748" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/2011-08-07_170748.jpg" alt="" width="486" height="392" /></a>

5.After the installation finishes, Doomi's main program opens automatically. The interface is very clean. Type what you want to record in the "Enter a to-do" box and press Enter to save it.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/2011-08-07_171223.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-5598" title="2011-08-07_171223" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/2011-08-07_171223.jpg" alt="" width="306" height="234" /></a>

6.If the item you entered doesn't appear after pressing Enter, Doomi may have hidden it. Click the down-arrow button next to "Enter a to-do" to show it.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/2011-08-07_171423.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-5599" title="2011-08-07_171423" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/2011-08-07_171423.jpg" alt="" width="314" height="62" /></a>

7.When you finish something, there's a checkbox on the left side of the item. Once you check it, the item will be hidden.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/2011-08-07_171614.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-5600" title="2011-08-07_171614" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/2011-08-07_171614.jpg" alt="" width="320" height="295" /></a>

To show completed items that have been hidden, click "Show Old."

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/2011-08-07_171748.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-5601" title="2011-08-07_171748" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/2011-08-07_171748.jpg" alt="" width="307" height="230" /></a>

8.You can also click an item you've recorded to edit it. Press Enter after editing to save.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/2011-08-07_172033.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-5602" title="2011-08-07_172033" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/2011-08-07_172033.jpg" alt="" width="309" height="217" /></a>

9.You can also click the small clock on the right side of an item to set how long you have to complete it.

P.S. This sets a time limit for completion ("within X hours"), not a specific date and time.

<h2>Thoughts</h2>
<div>Is a ToDoList really necessary? Some people find it troublesome and don't want to bother with this kind of task, but in my own experience, it's very handy. Human memory is limited. Many people have had the experience of trying to "remember" what they planned to do today: you plan to study in the morning and forget by lunch, or you plan to write an article tomorrow and forget after a nap. In those cases, you can record it in a calendar or a ToDoList. It may seem like the two overlap, but my own approach is to use the calendar for big, multi-day events, and a ToDoList for small tasks, like what you'll do this morning or this afternoon. A ToDoList is more convenient for those. But if it's something like a mid-term exam on a certain day, you should write it in the calendar!</div>
