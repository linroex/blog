<strong><a href="http://findbook.tw/">FindBook</a></strong> is a website that compares book prices for you. Just enter the book's title, the author's name, or its ISBN, and it lists which bookstores carry the book and at what price. It also has a "Daily 66% Zone" that collects books sold at 66% of the list price. I'd recommend that anyone who wants to buy books check prices there first. I've compared prices there myself, and the difference is actually quite big. For example, if a bookstore's list price is 500, FindBook can find it for as little as 300 (or even 250). FindBook also calculates shipping for you, which is really convenient~

<h2>FindBook Price Comparison Tutorial</h2>
1. Go to the <a href="http://findbook.tw/">FindBook</a> website, then type the book's title or ISBN into the search box. Either one works.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-02_111208.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3257" title="2010-06-02_111208" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-02_111208-543x400.jpg" alt="" width="543" height="400" /></a>

2. The system lists the books it found. Select the book you want to buy and open it.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-02_111514.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3258" title="2010-06-02_111514" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-02_111514-550x334.jpg" alt="" width="550" height="334" /></a>

3. The system shows the book's list price, its lowest price, and an introduction to the book. Click 【Compare Prices】 to see each bookstore's price and shipping fee.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-02_111900.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3259" title="2010-06-02_111900" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-02_111900-550x334.jpg" alt="" width="550" height="334" /></a>

4. Hover your mouse over 【Check It Out】 to see detailed shipping information. Click 【Check It Out】 to go to that bookstore's website.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-02_112250.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-3260" title="2010-06-02_112250" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/2010-06-02_112250-550x334.jpg" alt="" width="550" height="334" /></a>
<h2>My Thoughts:</h2>
I once bought a question bank for the Level C certification exam from here. I thought it would be about 50 dollars cheaper at first, but when I checked, it turned out to be more than 100 dollars cheaper! Buying books through FindBook is also safe, because FindBook doesn't handle any money. It only organizes which online bookstores have the book and what they charge. The actual purchase has to be made on that bookstore's site. By the way, while I was writing this tutorial, BU was also using this site to give away books, XD
