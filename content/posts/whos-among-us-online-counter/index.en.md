<strong> whos.amung.us</strong> is the best-known counter I know of. It can count how many people are online right now, and <strong>whos.amung.us</strong> can also tell you which posts those visitors are reading. Most importantly, <strong>whos.amung.us</strong> requires no registration and costs nothing. You don't have to give them any information to use their service.

URL: <a title="http://whos.amung.us/" href="http://whos.amung.us/">http://whos.amung.us/</a>


<!--more-->

<h3>How to Use whos.amung.us:</h3>
1. Go to the <a href="http://whos.amung.us/" target="_blank">whos.amung.us</a> website and scroll down. You'll find a block of code. Copy that code and paste it into your blog.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_165903.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border: 0px;" title="2009-10-04_165903" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_165903_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="2009-10-04_165903" width="354" height="379" /></a> (Click to view larger if it's hard to read.)

If your blog doesn't let you add JavaScript code (for example, Wretch), click "Non Javascript," then copy the code provided there instead.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_170132.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border: 0px;" title="2009-10-04_170132" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_170132_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="2009-10-04_170132" width="354" height="255" /></a> (Click to view larger if it's hard to read.)

I'm sure everyone knows how to add a sidebar on their own, so I won't go into that here. XD

Once you've installed whos.amung.us, click the whos.amung.us image on your blog. It will take you to another page that shows how many visitors are currently reading your blog and where they're coming from. (Click the image below and you'll see what I mean. XD)
