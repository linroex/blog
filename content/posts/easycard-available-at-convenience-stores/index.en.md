On April 1st, when I woke up, I had totally forgotten it was April Fools' Day, because that day there was something more important to me: I had a date... oops, typo! Actually, April 1st was the first day EasyCard could be used at the four major convenience stores (7-Eleven, FamilyMart, Hi-Life, and one more I can't remember = =). I had been waiting for this day since the Lunar New Year, because I don't like carrying a bunch of cards around: EasyCard, iCash, bank card, ID card, phone card... The weight of the cards alone is equal to two iPod Touches! Now I can combine my EasyCard and iCash with my wallet (which I mostly use at convenience stores), so my whole pocket is free. If I really need cash, I can just withdraw it with my bank card (though the most convenient option is a stored-value credit card, which is basically an e-wallet). Right now I'm trying to combine my EasyCard, my iPod Touch, and my library card. Maybe after the Basic Competence Test (the high school entrance exam), there will be a service that converts an iPod Touch into an EasyCard phone~~~

<h2>Partner merchants that accept EasyCard:</h2>
7-ELEVEN, FamilyMart, Hi-Life, OK, Starbucks, Tenren Tea, IS COFFEE, Watsons, Cosmed, Wellcome supermarket, Sung Ching supermarket, Miramar Cinemas, Dalu Ge Baseball and Softball Batting Range, Yamazaki, MisterDonut, Dunkin Donuts, Hsin Tung Yang, Cold Stone, Afternoon Tea, Domino's, Pizza Hut, Global Shopping Center (Banqiao food court), MUJI, Bread First

The EasyCard can currently hold a maximum of NT$10,000. That reminds me of when someone took several EasyCards to a 7-Eleven and topped up each one with several thousand dollars = = — but that's a digression. The main point is that you can apply for a registered card, so you don't have to worry about losing your card.

<h2>About Registered Cards:</h2>
Registered EasyCards are cards issued by the EasyCard company with personal information printed on them. The current types include registered EasyCards that the public can apply for on their own, community access EasyCards, company or institution ID EasyCards, student ID EasyCards, and original-design EasyCards. Unless the card itself is damaged and needs to be replaced, returning a card incurs a NT$20 administrative fee.
