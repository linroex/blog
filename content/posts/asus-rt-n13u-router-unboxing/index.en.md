I had some spare money lying around, and I'd put up with my old D-Link wireless router for a long time. It kept dropping the connection, and especially whenever my iPad opened the App Store, it would disconnect immediately = = So with both the motive and the budget in place, I decided to buy a new wireless router~ At first I thought about going to Guanghua (Taipei's electronics market), but then I figured it's easy to get ripped off there, and Guanghua generally doesn't accept returns. If the router I bought didn't meet my needs, I'd be furious. So I went browsing on PChome instead xD


<!--more-->


The reason I chose this ASUS RT-N13U is that the moment I entered PChome, its title hooked me: "A wireless router that does three jobs in one. It can also download BT and HTTP, and even act as an FTP server!" That makes it half a NAS, and the price was unbelievably low, only 16xx NT dollars. Later I searched online for articles about the ASUS RT-N13U and found the reviews were pretty good. The only drawback was weaker Chinese-language support, but that's no big deal, right!?

Also, the ASUS RT-N13U uses an internal antenna design, so the antennas aren't exposed! Some people might worry that the signal will be weak, but in my 30-ping (about 100 square meters) home, I get full signal bars everywhere~ So you don't need to worry about signal. It also has a built-in USB port. If you want to use FTP, downloads, and similar features, you must first connect a USB device to the N13U's main unit.

Finally, the ASUS RT-N13U has very strong management features. You can easily set up the firewall and set priorities for different types of packets. For example, you can give game packets top priority, so your game lags less when the network is saturated. The firewall section can also block specific network card (MAC) addresses from connecting, which is really handy. My younger brother sometimes stays online too late, so I can just cut off his network...

<strong>By the way: the default username and password are both admin, and the management interface is http://192.168.1.1</strong>

<h2>Afterthoughts:</h2>
My impression of the ASUS RT-N13U is.... its download features and FTP server really are a bit of a "chicken rib" (not worth much, but hard to give up). But as far as wireless goes, it's quite good. Downloads and FTP are just auxiliary features, and honestly they're pretty weak. BT downloads drove me crazy. The BT download speed is pitifully slow. If you give it a movie torrent, it won't even budge. Also, with the firmware I'm running now, the Chinese-language support is really poor. If your files or folders have Chinese names, the FTP feature is almost completely unusable... Luckily, it didn't weaken its main function! So overall, it's a good product that "didn't live up to expectations."
