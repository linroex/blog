jetable.org is a site that forwards emails for you. When you sign up on a website, it gives you a random-looking email address that you can use to register accounts or for other purposes. Emails go to their server first and are then forwarded to you, so your real email address is never exposed.

jetable.org features
<ul>
	<li>Free</li>
	<li>Supports multiple languages</li>
	<li>Expiration time can be customized</li>
</ul>

<!--more-->


Using it is very simple. Just follow the steps below.

1. Go to <a href="http://www.jetable.org/zh/index">jetable.org</a>

2. Enter your email address in the red box, choose how long you want to keep this disposable address under "Mailbox validity," and then click the button at the very bottom.

3. It will show a random-looking email address. Note it down, and then you can use it to sign up for accounts.

Summary: Some people may worry that the site will leak your information to advertisers. There's no need to worry about that. It's a service provided by APINC (a non-commercial internet organization), which is why you won't notice any ads on its pages XD. Personally, I think it's a great service!!
