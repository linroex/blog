In this age of information explosion, pirated software and music flood the internet. I think very few people buy official CDs to listen to music anymore. Instead, they go online and grab MP3s! In this post, I'll share a trick you really should know: how to grab music straight from Yam Tiankong. You might ask, "Can't you already grab music from Yam Tiankong?" True, in some ways you can't. But now we have a site called GooTube FLV, which parses out the real address of Yam Tiankong's audio and video, so you can grab its music!

P.S. GooTube FLV also works for downloading YouTube videos.

URL <a href="http://kej.tw/flvretriever/">http://kej.tw/flvretriever/</a>
<h2>
<!--more-->
GooTube FLV Tutorial</h2>
1. First, copy the URL of the music you want.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/04/2009-10-31-16-41-55.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-2148" title="2009-10-31 16 41 55" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/04/2009-10-31-16-41-55.jpg" alt="2009-10-31 16 41 55" width="494" height="202" /></a>

2. Paste the URL into the "Video URL" box in GooTube FLV, then click "REVRITE NOW!"

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/04/2009-10-31-16-45-01.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-2149" title="2009-10-31 16 45 01" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/04/2009-10-31-16-45-01.jpg" alt="2009-10-31 16 45 01" width="480" height="207" /></a>

3. Wait a moment for GooTube FLV to process, and the music's address will appear in the "FLV video file URL" field.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/04/2009-10-31-16-47-59.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-2150" title="2009-10-31 16 47 59" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/04/2009-10-31-16-47-59.jpg" alt="2009-10-31 16 47 59" width="449" height="247" /></a>

Now you can download it with a program like FlashGet. If you use multi-threaded downloading, I recommend setting the number of simultaneous downloads to 3 or fewer, or Yam Tiankong's servers may block you.
