<h2>Imgburn功能</h2>
<ol>
	<li>燒錄檔案到CD/DVD</li>
	<li>將檔案製作成ISO</li>
	<li>將光碟製作成ISO</li>
	<li>將ISO燒錄到CD/DVD</li>
</ol>
<h2>一.Imgburn安裝教學</h2>
1.雙擊Imgburn安裝檔,如果出現安全性警告就不要理它,直接按【執行】

2.按【Next】

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/01/2010-01-22_194754.png"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2527" title="2010-01-22_194754" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/01/2010-01-22_194754-500x384.png" alt="" width="500" height="384" /></a>

3.設定要安裝的類型,基本上不用去動這塊,直接按【Next】

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/01/2010-01-22_194912.png"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2528" title="2010-01-22_194912" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/01/2010-01-22_194912-500x384.png" alt="" width="500" height="384" /></a>

4.設定安裝路徑,除非你的硬碟空間不夠要裝到其他地方,不然就直接按【Next】

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/01/2010-01-22_195047.png"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2529" title="2010-01-22_195047" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/01/2010-01-22_195047-500x384.png" alt="" width="500" height="384" /></a>

5.直接按【Install】

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/01/2010-01-22_195222.png"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2530" title="2010-01-22_195222" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/01/2010-01-22_195222-500x384.png" alt="" width="500" height="384" /></a>

6.安裝完成,按【Finish】就可以開啟Imgburn了

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/01/2010-01-22_195548.png"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2531" title="2010-01-22_195548" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/01/2010-01-22_195548-500x384.png" alt="" width="500" height="384" /></a>

7.接下來要教各位安裝正體中文的翻譯檔,首先請開啟Imgburn所在的資料夾,如果你剛剛沒有修改安裝位置的話都是『C:Program FilesImgBurn』,進入到Imgburn所在的資料以後再進入一個叫『Languages』的資料夾

8.把Imgburn的翻譯檔複製到『Languages』裡面然後開啟Imgburn主程式
<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/01/2010-01-22_200312.png"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2533" title="2010-01-22_200312" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/01/2010-01-22_200312-500x326.png" alt="" width="500" height="326" /></a>

9.開啟Imgburn主程式以後,依序點選上方工具列的【Tools→settings】

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/01/2010-01-22_200550.png"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2534" title="2010-01-22_200550" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/01/2010-01-22_200550-500x344.png" alt="" width="500" height="344" /></a>

10.選擇頁籤【General→Page 1】然後把【Languages】改成【繁體中文】再按【OK】就會變成中文介面了

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/01/2010-01-22_200851.png"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-2535" title="2010-01-22_200851" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2010/01/2010-01-22_200851-415x400.png" alt="" width="415" height="400" /></a>

<h2>三.把CD製成映像檔(ISO)</h2>
