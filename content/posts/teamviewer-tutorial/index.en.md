<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_123651_thumb.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border: 0px initial initial;" title="2009-10-04_123651" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_123651_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="2009-10-04_123651" width="504" height="331" /></a>

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_123858.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border: 0px;" title="2009-10-04_123858" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_123858_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="2009-10-04_123858" width="507" height="397" /></a>

3. Select the first option (personal......), then click Next.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_124132.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border: 0px;" title="2009-10-04_124132" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_124132_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="2009-10-04_124132" width="507" height="397" /></a>

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_124221.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border: 0px;" title="2009-10-04_124221" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_124221_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="2009-10-04_124221" width="507" height="397" /></a>

<h3>2. Configuration Guide</h3>
I hope everyone has installed TeamViewer from the previous section! After installation, we need to make a few basic settings to make it more convenient to use.

1. Click "Extras" → "Options" in order.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_125021.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border: 0px;" title="2009-10-04_125021" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_125021_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="2009-10-04_125021" width="504" height="350" /></a>

2. In the General section, enter your nickname, and in the Password field, enter the password you want. When someone connects to you, they will need to enter this password.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_125417.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border: 0px;" title="2009-10-04_125417" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_125417_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="2009-10-04_125417" width="504" height="366" /></a>

After setting the name and password above, switch to the Security tab and check both options under General. This prevents others from messing with your computer's settings.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_125809.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border: 0px;" title="2009-10-04_125809" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_125809_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="2009-10-04_125809" width="504" height="366" /></a>

Once you have made these settings, click OK to save the data.
<h3>3. Remote Connection Guide</h3>
Phew~ Now we finally get to the main topic: how to use TeamViewer to remotely control someone else's computer! First, get the ID and password of the controlled computer. Then enter the ID in the ID field under Create Session, and click Connect to partner.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_131043.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border: 0px;" title="2009-10-04_131043" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_131043_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="2009-10-04_131043" width="504" height="350" /></a>

Next, a password input window will appear. Enter the password the other person gave you (not your own password), then click Log On.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_131101.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border: 0px;" title="2009-10-04_131101" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_131101_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="2009-10-04_131101" width="387" height="190" /></a>

OK~ Success! You can now see the other person's desktop. Go ahead and move the mouse!

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_131117.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border: 0px;" title="2009-10-04_131117" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_131117_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="2009-10-04_131117" width="504" height="394" /></a>
<h4>How do I get the ID and password?</h4>
For the password, the second section showed you how to set it yourself. Just give that password to the other person. As for the ID, you will see it as soon as you open the program.

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_131329.jpg"><img style="display: inline; border: 0px;" title="2009-10-04_131329" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/20091004_131329_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="2009-10-04_131329" width="504" height="350" /></a>

Afterword: Besides the features covered above, TeamViewer has many other ways to use it that you can explore on your own, such as the recording feature in the new version. This article only covers the most commonly used features. Some people may ask why I did not use a Chinese version of the program for the tutorial. The reason is simple: Chinese versions come in many editions, and each person's translation is different. And since everyone already uses the Chinese program, there is nothing much to explain.
