<h2>Recording Tutorial:</h2>
1. Extract the archive, then open the main program, Freez Screen Video Capture.exe

2. Click [Options] to set up hotkeys

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-04_105706.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1521" title="2009-07-04_105706" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-04_105706.jpg" alt="2009-07-04_105706" width="387" height="174" /></a>

3. Set your hotkeys in the hotkey section. Check "Minimize while recording". If you want slightly better video quality, you can also change other properties. When you're done, click [Close]

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-04_105856.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1522" title="2009-07-04_105856" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-04_105856.jpg" alt="2009-07-04_105856" width="469" height="486" /></a>

4. Now you can start recording. Press the recording hotkey you just set, and it will ask you to select the recording area. Personally, I always select the entire screen

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-04_110322.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1523" title="2009-07-04_110322" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-04_110322.jpg" alt="2009-07-04_110322" width="299" height="238" /></a>

5. When you finish recording, press the stop hotkey you set, and a window will pop up asking where you want to save the file

<a href="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-04_110553.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1524" title="2009-07-04_110553" src="{{< param imageBase >}}/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/2009-07-04_110553.jpg" alt="2009-07-04_110553" width="401" height="270" /></a>

Note: the first time you use it, it will ask you to update. Just click [Cancel].

Summary: this program is really excellent. Although it's free, its features are by no means inferior. The recorded video quality isn't bad either. Unlike many other free recording programs that stamp a watermark on your videos, which you can remove but only with a hassle, this one doesn't. It's also a portable (green) program, so you can put it on a USB drive and take it out whenever you need it.
